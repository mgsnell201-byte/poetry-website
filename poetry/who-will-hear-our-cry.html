Poem · No. 446

Who Will Hear Our Cry?

What secrets lie in mind's dark maze, Where wisdom sleeps through countless days? Which path among these twisted ways Will lead us through this shadowed haze? How did two hearts, once joined as one, Lose all the grace they'd proudly won? Through love's maze now, separate they run, Till passion's dance came all undone. Why must bullets fly astray, To steal an innocent child away? What words can soothe a father's pain, When senseless violence leaves such stain? Can time mend what violence tears? Who heals the wounds of passing years? Through endless halls we searching go— Who will answer? Do we know? Like wanderers in fate's design, Through corridors of space and time, Beneath the city's ceaseless roar, What silent truths do we ignore? When screams are lost in neon light, Who hears the whispers in the night? Through mazes made of steel and stone, Who guides us when we're lost, alone? Could ancient tomes reveal the way? Do hermits' cells hold light of day? Might wisdom rest in solitude Where silence speaks in sacred mood? Is truth heard in wind's soft sigh? Or written in the starlit sky? When will we find the peace we seek? Which voice of wisdom will finally speak? Through labyrinths of doubt and fear, Whose light will guide when none seems near? Will heart's pulse lead us through the night, Till dawn breaks with its answer bright? When earthly voices fade away, And mortal wisdom fails to say, Could heaven's eternal, loving eyes Be watching as we seek reply?
