Thank's! 'No Worries'

In this world of hollow courtesies, I offer thanks, sincere and unrestrained, You toss back "No worries!" with callous ease, As if my gratitude were feigned or strained. My words of appreciation, carefully weighed, Deserve more than your flippant, trite retort, Your casual dismissal leaves me dismayed, As if my sentiment were naught but sport. Is genuine thankfulness now passé? A relic in this age of glib exchange? If "no worries" is all you deign to say, I'll reserve my thanks for those less strange. Your nonchalance is grating, I confess, Next time, expect no warmth from me, my friend, I'll match your apathy with coolness, yes, And let this dance of empty words now end.
