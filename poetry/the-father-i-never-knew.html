The Father I Never Knew

Verse 1 Found an old shoebox hidden in the attic one day Filled with black and white photos and letters tied with string One was to my momma, dated just a week away From when his plane went down, from when he stopped dreaming A picture of him in uniform, smiling so proud He never met me, but I've heard the stories aloud Chorus I raise my hands, look to the sky I'm finding more and more truth in the love that never dies They tell me you're with me in every step that I take Oh, I believe. Verse 2 Grandma says I have your eyes, and your stubborn way Of whistling that tune you wrote the night before you flew She saved your guitar, it's in the shed, she says it's okay To go and play it, that I’ve got your talent too Uncle Joe said you cried the day I was born You taped the nurse's call, listened to me breathe You promised you'd come home, you'd never be gone But war doesn't keep promises, it only leaves Chorus I raise my hands, look to the sky I'm finding more and more truth in the love that never dies They tell me you're with me in every step that I take Oh, I believe. Verse 3 Tonight I played that song you started writing Added a verse, imagined you were near I can almost feel you, softly listening Like you're right here, Dad, wherever you are, I hear Chorus I raise my hands, look to the sky I'm finding more and more truth in the love that never dies They tell me you're with me in every step that I take Oh, I believe. Bridge I can't hear your voice Or feel your hand in mine You can't tell me this pain Is just a part of time You know I'm more and more convinced The longer that I live Yeah, this can't be No, this can't be No, this can't be all there is Chorus / Outro When I raise my hands, look to the sky I'm finding more and more truth in the love that never dies They tell me you're with me in every step that I take I believe. Oh, I I believe. I believe. I believe. I believe. I believe...
