The Wait

In line we stand, a common fate, Our patience tested as we wait. A queue for coffee, bus, or bread, Time crawls by, dripping overhead. Phones in hand, we scroll and sigh, Watching minutes shuffle by. Strangers linked by shared delay, United in this slow ballet. Fidgeting feet and darting eyes, We're all alike beneath these skies. Rich or poor, young or old, In waiting's grip, we're equally bold. Progress inches, hope renews, Each step forward good news. At last, our turn, the wait complete, Life resumes its hurried beat. Yet in this pause, a truth unfolds: We're more alike than stories told. In patience found, or patience lost, We share this wait, at any cost.
