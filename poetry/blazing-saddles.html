Blazing Saddles

Two middle-aged men with boyish grins, Embarked on pranks that reeked of sins. Armed with lighters, beans, and brew, They sought a flame of greenish hue. "On three," said one, "We'll light the gas!" But timing's not their strongest class. A rumble grew, then with a "BOOM!" It shook the walls of every room. Singed and startled, they stumbled out, Their neighbors gawked and began to shout. "What's that smell?" came concerned inquiries, While our duo nursed their seared posteriors. With pants ablaze and pride in tatters, They learned why safety always matters. For men who play with fire and flatulence, May find themselves in need of ambulance! So let this tale a warning be, To those who'd test gastronomy: Some childhood dares are best left alone, Lest your behind becomes well-known!
