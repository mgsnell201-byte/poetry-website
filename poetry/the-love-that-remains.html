The Love That Remains

I loved you, but the love I gave, Was not a chain that makes a slave, It was a seed I chose to sow, In soil where I hoped it would grow. I loved you, but I love me more, Than I had ever loved before, I love the person I must be, The one you never stopped to see. I loved you, but my heart's not stone, It learned the way to stand alone, It found its rhythm in the ache, And learned the difference hearts can make. I loved you, but the chapter's turned, A lesson tenderly unlearned, The love I gave was not in vain, It now becomes a falling rain. That waters roots I'm coming to, A stronger, more enduring truth, For what I feel for my own soul, Is making finally, finally whole.
