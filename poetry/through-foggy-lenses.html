Through Foggy Lenses

A spectacles' whisper, soft and low, Through foggy lenses, truths appear. The world's a blur, yet hearts bestow Like clouds that hide what we hold dear. I read the faces in the crowd, With eyes that once saw every line. Now I watch their movements proud, A dance of shadows, not sunshine. The stars, they shimmer, but I know Each one's a face I've seen before. The moon, a ghost, but I can show The stories of its silent lore. I laugh at how the world can fade, But I see souls in every face. Like autumn leaves that fall and stayed, Yet hold the warmth of summer's grace. The clock ticks on, but I still see The hidden signs that time has made. A joke, a sigh, a glance so free— The old world's secrets now displayed. I'm not just older, I'm wise and true, In every blur, I find what's right. The eyes may fail, but hearts break through To see the world in its purest light.
