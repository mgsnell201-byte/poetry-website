Quiet, Can You Hear It?

Quiet, can you hear it now? The whisper underneath the noise, A gentle breeze against your brow, A soft and steady, tender voice. It speaks of all the things you are, Not loud enough to shake the sky, But constant as the farthest star, A truth that doesn't need to fly. It's in the space between your tears, The pause before you catch your breath, The stillness that has held your fears, The quiet life that conquers death. Can you hear your heart at rest? The beauty in the silent spring, The strength you carry in your chest, The song that only you can sing. It doesn't roar, it doesn't shout, It settles like the winter snow, A knowing deep within, throughout— The quiet you already know. So listen to the voice inside, The one that's always been your home, It whispers where the truth can hide: You've never really been alone.
