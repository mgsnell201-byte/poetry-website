Tom the Cat

Tom struts down the alley each day, With whiskers all groomed in his way. He preens by the trash, Adjusts his mou-stache— The ladies all purr, "Hip hoo-ray!" His friends think he's quite a de-light, They gather 'round Tom every night. He smooths down his fur, Adds one extra purr, And practices looking just right. He polishes each little paw, The alley cats all watch him with awe. "Such style!" they all say, "Tom's dapper all day!" He's fashion's most purr-fect outlaw. With tail held up high in the air, Tom knows that he's beauty-cat rare. The girl cats all swoon, He hums his own tune— A dandy without any care!
