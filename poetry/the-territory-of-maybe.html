The Territory of Maybe

There is a country between the way you looked at me this morning— coffee steam rising, your eyes soft as apology— and the way you look at me now: flint striking steel across the dinner table. In that territory lives everything that happened and didn't happen, the archaeology of hours where love turned its back just long enough for rage to slip through the unlocked door. What lives there? The unanswered text at 2:47 PM. The way you said "fine" like a period at the end of us. The moment I chose silence over truth, thinking it would cost less. Between tenderness and fury stretches a no-man's land where all our small betrayals grow into something with teeth, where the distance from pupil to pupil becomes a canyon I didn't know we were digging with our careful words, our polite omissions, our stubborn refusal to say what we mean when we still had time to mean it.
