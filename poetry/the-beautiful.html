The Beautiful .

I want to tell you about the period, that small black dot we've been placing at the end of our sentences since grade school, how it sits there like a tiny stone that holds everything in place. You might think I'm talking about punctuation, and I am, but I'm also talking about the period at the end of summer when the light begins to slant differently and you notice the first yellow leaf spinning down from the maple tree. Or the period at the end of a phone call with someone you love, that moment when you've both said goodbye but neither of you has hung up yet, and you can hear them breathing on the other end of the line. There's a period at the end of childhood, though you don't see it coming— one day you're riding your bike down the center of the street with no hands, and the next day you're worried about the mortgage. I think about the period my father placed at the end of his stories, the way he'd pause and look at us around the dinner table, making sure we understood before moving on to dessert. Now there's a period at the end of his voice, which I can only hear in my memory, getting fainter each year. But here's what makes the period beautiful: it's not really an ending, is it? It's more like a breath between thoughts, a momentary pause before the next sentence rushes in with its cargo of words. The period is the shape of completeness, a perfect circle, nothing missing, nothing left unsaid—or at least nothing more that needs saying right now, at this particular instant. I suppose that's why calligraphers take such care with their periods, pressing the nib down just so, making that small dark pool of ink that says: here, this is finished, this idea has come to rest, you can stop holding your breath now. And maybe that's what makes any ending beautiful— not the finality of it, but the way it gives us permission to pause, to let something settle, before we lean forward again into the mystery of the next sentence, which is already forming on our lips, already reaching toward its own necessary and beautiful conclusion.
