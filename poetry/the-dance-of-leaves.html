The Dance of Leaves

As autumn whispers, leaves drift down, Embracing earth, their cozy bed, In the cool air, they wear a crown, Trading the heights for a slumber spread. The trees, with moss, begin to dream, Of birds that soon will find their place, Nestled in branches, a gentle team, Warmed by the bark’s tender embrace. Goldenrod bows, a final sigh, Snowflakes gather, soft and light, Whispering tales as the day bids goodbye, Covering fields in a blanket white. The pond retreats into twilight’s grasp, While shadows leap as the fox darts fast, In the fading light, time’s gentle clasp, Marking the moments, wishing they’d last. And evening whispers to wood piled high, A fire’s soft glow, a flickering gaze, Longing for warmth beneath the sky, As the quiet night fills the world with haze.
