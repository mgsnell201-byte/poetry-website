Dolly's Morning Splash

I am Dolly, queen of the sparkling sea, and dawn calls me to race the rising sun. My fins flick, slicing waves that pirouette, as I leap, a silver arc against the sky’s blush. The coral reef hums, a city of color— pink anemones sway, clownfish dart like giggles. I whistle to my pod, “Catch me if you can!” and we surge through seaweed, a twisting, twirling parade. Sunlight scatters like jewels on the water’s skin, cool currents kiss my sides, tickling as I spin. A turtle cheers, slow and wise, from a sandy throne, while starfish applaud with their silent, starry glow. We chase a school of fish, their scales a silver storm, my heart drums fast, alive with the ocean’s song. Through a cave’s mouth, I glide, shadows dancing on stone, and pop out to splash, spraying rainbows to the foam. My pod’s clicks echo, a symphony of glee, we weave tales of adventure, of waves and liberty. In this playground of tides, I’m home, I’m free— Dolly, the dolphin, splashing joy across the sea.
