When You Broke Me: I Loved

When you broke me, the fault line carved its way, Through laughter's echo, through dreams we used to say. Each shard a memory, sharp and glittering pain, Yet in my chest, where love had lived, it bloomed again. No fury scorched, no vengeful words took flight, Just tear-stained whispers in the fading light. For hate's a barren field, where nothing grows, And I, my love, would rather tend a rose. I see the shadows in your tired eyes, The weight you carry, beneath your gentle guise. And though it tore me, shattered piece by piece, My love won't crumble, it finds release. So let us tend these embers, hand in hand, Though paths diverge, love's flame transcends the land. For broken hearts, that choose to understand, Find solace, not in blame, but in a gentler brand. I'll hold your fragments, each one precious lore, And whisper, "Love you still," when silence roars. For though you broke me, my love did not depart, It blooms anew, with an open, tender heart.
