Age A Reflection

Fade, the vibrant colors of youth, Succumb to soft shades of reality's truth. In obsidian mirrors and silken strands gray, We glimpse our dawn transition to day. Skin once kissed by morning dewdrops light Now tells tales in wrinkles' quiet night. Eyes that sparkled with unclouded view Begin a dance with time -- A rendezvous. Yet within those echoes lives wisdom deep, Life’s stories etched where fleeting moments seep, Lessons learned from emerald years now past- Our reflections imbued by shadows cast, Silver threads woven through memory's loom-- Echoes of laughter fill life’s sunlit room Mellowing fine like seasoned wood are we. Age is not just decline; it's eternity free. So celebrate then this journey long made- Every sunset hue, all traces left in shade. For age cloaks us not in gloominess plight - It adorns us instead...in tender twilight.
