Lamb-bent

Between the dark and light, Between the day and night, The sky goes shimmer-soft, The colors swirl aloft— Not blue, Not black, Not quite this, Not quite that. A bird? A bat? A tweeter? A squeaker? The dawn gets weaker-stronger, The dusk stays longer-shorter. Almost-words on almost-lips, Doorway pauses, Threshold trips— Standing in the in-between, Half-seen, Dream-screen, Might-have-been! Caterpillar wriggle-waiting, Teenager hesitating, Friendship maybe-mating— Everything that's Glimmer-glowing, Never quite All-the-way showing, Flicker-light, Shimmer-bright, Lamb-bent Through the night!
