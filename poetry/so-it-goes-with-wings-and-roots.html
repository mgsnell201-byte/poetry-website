So It Goes with Wings and Roots

A painted wing seeks something sweet, A flower waits on rooted feet, The dancer lands, the holder sways, And so they spend their summer days. The thirsty one drinks deep and long, The patient one breathes dusk like song, A drifting scent through evening air, A fragrance hanging soft and fair. The restless one keeps fluttering wide, The rooted one holds earth with pride, But neither minds the way things are, One staying close, one flying far. They need each other, that's the trick, The still one steadies the one that's quick, The one who moves, the one who stays, Connected in peculiar ways. One gathers glimmers from the skies, One grounds them where the quiet lies, And through the bond of wing and root A deeper meaning finds its route.
