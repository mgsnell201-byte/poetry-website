Vigil at the Manger

Beneath the window’s trembling light The hush of evening sways the air, Where hay and straw recall the night A colt was born without despair. A horse stands patient near the stall, His breath—mist soft against the cold; No stallion’s kingly trumpets call, Yet here his warmth feels brave, feels bold. Light glints off frozen fence and pine, The manger holds its quiet claim; Soft bells, unstrung, recall the shrine, While angels mute still speak his name. He does not gallop far or flee; His freedom found in this small room; Against the dark he still might be— The spark that glows beyond the gloom.
