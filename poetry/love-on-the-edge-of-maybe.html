Love on the Edge of Maybe

Intro You trace my hand Like shifting sand But every line Keeps realigning Verse 1 We talk in circles Coffee gone cold You say you're "fine" Eyes already told Laugh in the doorway Like nothing's wrong Dodging the questions We've dodged so long Pre-Chorus Are we just almost there Are we just standing near Why does every heartbeat Keep asking if you're here Chorus Love on the edge of maybe Halfway brave, halfway scared I'm falling, but are you falling Or just standing, staying there Say you want me, say you don't I'll be here when shadows blur Love on the edge of maybe Hurts the most when it feels like her Verse 2 You leave your sweater On my chair Like some small promise You're almost there We start a movie Never see it through Paused in the middle Are we more than two Pre-Chorus Every near-miss Every "not yet" Feels like lightning Before we get wet Chorus Love on the edge of maybe Halfway brave, halfway scared I'm falling, but are you falling Or just standing, staying there Say you want me, say you don't I'll be here when shadows blur Love on the edge of maybe Hurts the most when it feels like her Bridge Tell me the truth Even if it breaks me Better the fall Than this almost-safety I'd rather hear "stay" Or I'd rather hear "go" Than live in the middle Of "I don't know" Final Chorus Love on the edge of maybe Halfway brave, halfway scared I'm falling, but are you falling Or just standing, staying there Say you want me, say you don't I'll be here when shadows blur Love on the edge of maybe Hurts the most when it feels like her Outro You trace my hand Just like before This time the line Runs to your door If you say "stay" Say it true I'll trade my fear To be with you
