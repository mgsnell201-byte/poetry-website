Bug's Big Night

For ages I've been slapped and swatted, chased from picnics, doors slam-shutted, rolled-up papers whacked my kin— but tonight the fun begins! They sent a card with glitter glue, "Dear Mosquito, come join the crew!" Red carpet rolled across the lawn, standing ovation—me, the dawn! No swatter waves, no screams of "Eek!", they clap and cheer, they kiss my cheek, they pour me punch (a tiny cup), and toast the buzz I've stirred them up. I land on cake, they shout "Hooray!", the band plays tunes in my own way— but wait—oh clever twist so sweet— they've lit the bug-zap light to greet!
