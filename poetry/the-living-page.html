The Living Page

A butterfly, like a magic page, Danced where the meadow gently rolled, Each wing-flick turning nature's stage To stories waiting to unfold. Each graceful swoop across the light Released new words into the air, While letters shimmered, swift and bright, As morning's ink flowed free and fair. I reached to catch the floating text, My small hands trembling with the thrill, Not knowing which word would come next Upon this page that wouldn't still. The sentences swirled high and low, Beyond my eager, searching eyes, This magic page continued flow, Its story told in wing-swept skies. Now when I spot a butterfly, I see that same enchanted page, Still writing tales as it glides by, A magic that defies each age.
