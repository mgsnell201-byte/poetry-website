The Politician and the Lawyer: A Chat

The slippery pol with forked tongue so slick, Met a sharp-suited shark—oh, the rhetoric! With a handshake so firm and a smile so wide, They circled like wolves, each stuffed with pride. "My dear counselor," the spin doctor grinned, "Justice is blind, but loopholes, my friend!" The legal eagle scoffed, "Spare me your spiel, I bill by the hour—let’s make a deal!" "Ah!" cried the grandstander, "But voters adore me! I promise the moon—and then some, you see!" The ambulance chaser just rolled his eyes, "Save the hot air—billable hours don’t lie!" They quibbled and quibbled, each twisting the truth, One weasel-worded, one proof-proof-proof! Till the pollster’s puppet cried, "This is absurd! Let’s blame the press—no one heard what we heard!" The slick litigator just flashed a grin, "Or sue them for slander—cha-ching! We win!" So they backroom-dealt, shook hands (with a fee), And left us all wondering—who’s screwing ME? Moral: A pol and a lawyer walk into a bar… …and somehow, we get the tab! Bizarre!
