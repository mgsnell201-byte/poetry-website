Nature's Balance: A Chipmunk's Tale

Tiny paws on withered ground, I scamper, seeking what can't be found. Once lush and green, now brown and bare, My forest home in deep despair. Acorns scarce and berries few, I wonder, "What's a chipmunk to do?" Hunger gnaws, my cheeks deflate, Is this to be my forest's fate? I fear that winter is drawing near, No stores to last, oh dear, oh dear! What unseen force has changed my world? Has nature's balance been unfurled? Beyond my burrow I must go, To seek the truth, to learn and know. Though just a chipmunk, I'll play my part, To heal the forest's ailing heart. But wait! What's this? A sprout of green! A sign that all's not as it seems. Hope springs eternal, life renews, Perhaps my fears were misconstrued. So I'll keep faith and persevere, For brighter days may soon appear. Though times are tough, I'll brave this test, And trust that nature knows what's best.
