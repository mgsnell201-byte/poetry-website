A Wild Little Mix-Up

A wild little wasp with a watermelon hat Attempted to waltz with a very wry cat. “I wish you would whistle,” the wasp told her friend, “And promise to misbehave right to the end.” The cat gave a wink—then the W turned to M; A mink took its place. What a fix for them! It stole the last waffle and climbed up a chair, Leaving a whisper of crumbs in the air. “How weird!” cried the wasp. “There’s a wrinkle in things! That mink has my waffle; the cat has my wings!” Then wild became mild with one switch of a letter, And everyone’s manners grew suddenly better. They shared what was left, and the cat poured the tea. “One letter,” she said, “makes a new sort of me. But bring back the wild when we’ve finished this spread— I’d rather go dancing than toddle to bed!”
