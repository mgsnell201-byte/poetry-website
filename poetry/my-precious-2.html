My Precious

A decision needs to be made, a choice to unfold, From the many items on my list, which one to hold? I ponder and weigh, the pros and cons of each one, But as I deliberate, a thought begins to have fun. It whispers in my ear, a tantalizing suggestion, That makes me question, my initial intention. The items on my list, they all seem so fine, Each one a promise, of a memory that will shine. And then it hits me, a revelation so bright, I don't have to choose just one, I can choose the whole sight. I'll take the bucket, and all its contents with glee, And fulfill my bucket list, in one grand spree.
