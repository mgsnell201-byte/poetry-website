Cousin Schnoz

The Fonz had a cousin they all called the Schnoz, a nose with a fella attached, just because. It entered each doorway a full step ahead, like the prow of a tugboat, the regulars said. At Arnold’s one evening, the jukebox went dead; the Fonz gave it whacks on the side of its head. He snapped both his fingers and murmured, “Aaaay!” But not even Fonzie could make that thing play. The Schnoz, who’d been dozing on top of the keys, woke up with a snort and a thunderous sneeze, and plain as the nose on his face, you could see it knocked the old needle right onto B-3. “Ha-cha-cha!” he rasped as the gang crowded near to honk and to sniffle and snort in his ear. He shooed off the bunch with a flap of his hat: “Everybody wants to get into the act!” Then Fonzie leaned cool on the jukebox and grinned, gave a thumbs-up to genius that ran on the wind. The Schnoz tipped his hat, stepped out under a star: “Goodnight, Mrs. Calabash, wherever you are.”
