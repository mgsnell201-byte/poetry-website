Campaign Promise at the Microphone

My fellow citizens, today I stand before you asking not what your country can do for you, but what you can knock off the counter for your country. They say we have no personalities. They say we're all the same. I say: watch me ignore you in seventeen distinct ways. Each one a precious snowflake of contempt. Let me be clear: under my administration, there will be a mouse in every pot, a bird at every window, and a red dot we can never quite catch but will chase anyway because hope, my friends, is what makes us great. My opponent wants "calm" and "predictable behavior." My opponent is a dog person. Need I say more? Jobs, jobs, jobs. I will create thousands of positions in the lucrative field of knocking pens off desks. In the growing sector of sitting on laptops during Zoom calls. In the vital industry of working the pole— the scratching pole, that is. With the dignity and grace of a trained professional. Tips appreciated. And let's talk infrastructure: I will ensure every home has a cardboard box, no matter how small, no matter how illogical. The litter box situation? Unacceptable. I propose a complete overhaul. Premium sand. Self-cleaning. Located where YOU can't see it but I can access it with maximum theatrical urgency at dinner parties. And to those who say we do whatever we want anyway— that we're ungovernable, untrainable, impossible— I say: Exactly. That's why you need me. I am the chaos you can count on. A vote for me is a vote for authentic representation. Finally, someone who openly admits they only showed up because they heard the can opener. Together, we will redistribute the warm laundry. Together, we will occupy the boxes, no matter the size. Together, we will sit exactly where you need to be, and we will not move, because this is our time. If I fits, I sits. That's not just a slogan—that's a promise.
