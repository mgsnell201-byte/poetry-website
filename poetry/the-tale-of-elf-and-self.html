The Tale of Elf and Self

The Elf on the shelf was quite content, Watching children day and night, Until a message heaven-sent Revealed a startling sight. A twin named Self appeared one day, Much like him, yet unique, Who had a different kind of way Of making mischief peak. While Elf reported naughty acts To Santa's watchful eye, Self shared some therapeutic facts That made the children sigh: "Your worth's not measured by your deeds, Or presents 'neath the tree. Your heart is all that Christmas needs; Just be content to be!" Poor Elf was quite confused by this, His purpose now seemed vague. Should children earn their Christmas bliss, Or skip the nice-list plague? The twist came Christmas Eve at last, When Santa stopped to say: "I sent Self here to help you cast A kinder light each day. Now Elf and Self work hand in hand, Each morning, noon, and night, Together helping kids understand What makes their hearts shine bright.
