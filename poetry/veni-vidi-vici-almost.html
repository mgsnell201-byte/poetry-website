Poem · No. 314

Veni, vidi, vici (Almost)

I came—I saw my urgent need, A sudden, deep and crucial deed. I saw the roll, so plump and white, A beacon in my darkest night. I came to conquer Nature’s call, To stand up brave and never fall. I saw the paper, soft and sheer, The answer to my every fear. I came to claim my rightful throne, And do my business all alone. I saw—with panic and with dread— A single square was left. You read. I came, I saw, I… let out cries. I saw the cardboard, bare, despised. I came to vanquish, but instead, I had to waddle off to bed. So, Caesar, keep your ancient cheer, My victory's not even near. I came, I saw, I came undone… And yelled, "Which @#$% monster used ALL BUTT ONE?!"
