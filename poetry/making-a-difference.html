Making a Difference

My day was unscheduled, nothing was planned, So I walked to the beach to put my feet in the sand. The breeze was warm, gently guiding my way, The gulls in the air inviting me to stay. They beckoned me on to follow their flight, And along the way I saw a awful sight. The shore was littered as far as one could see, With hundreds of starfish cast from the sea. The day before, a tempest raged, fierce and wild, Tossing the waves and churning the sea, nature’s angry child. The wind roared and ocean frothed, a dance of wrath and fear, And many starfish lost their homes, their fate cruel and unclear. With waves crashing down, they were scattered far from shore, The fragile creatures tossed aside, cast out forevermore. Yet in this chaos, hope arose, in a form so small and bright, A little boy who saw their plight, determined to make things right. And as I approached the boy, this question I did ask, "For what purpose are you under taking this impossible task. You can't make a difference, you can't save them all, Time is your enemy and will be your downfall." Looking back at me with a smile and a grin, He turned and threw one more into the sea again. Then with the satisfaction of a job well done, He proudly stated with head held high, "I made a difference to that one!" And it was in that moment that it was clear for me to see, This little boy, was much wiser than me. With his youthful innocence clearly on display, He made a difference to each starfish he touched that day.
