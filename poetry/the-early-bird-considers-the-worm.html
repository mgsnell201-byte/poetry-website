The Early Bird Considers the Worm

They say the Dawn—rewards the Keen— But what of Those who slumber on? The Worm, unearthed before its Time, Might wish it had—stayed Home— I watched a Robin, proud with Prey, While Sluggards dreamt in Beds of Down— Yet something in that writhing Prize Made Victory—seem less a Crown— The Early Riser claims the Prize— But who would be the Worm? Perhaps the Glory lies— In knowing when to turn— To rise—or rest—what Wisdom says The Conqueror has won the Day? When Dawn breaks cold—and Beds are warm— I think I'd rather—stay.
