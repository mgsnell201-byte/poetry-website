Elegy for the Wolf

I have seen the river where you drank the stone is still there the stone does not know you are gone I have walked to the place in the trees where the dark held the shape of you where the grass still bends as though something passed through it as though something is still passing I have seen the rain come down onto the ground where you ran the ground takes it all the ground remembers nothing you did not know the last time was the last time you lifted your face to the dark the way you always had and the dark opened the way it always had and you went in I am standing at the edge of that I am standing where the river goes on without the sound of you the water does not grieve it crosses the same stones it goes where water goes I have come to say your name here at the river where no one is left to hear it
