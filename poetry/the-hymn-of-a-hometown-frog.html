The Hymn of a Hometown Frog

In murky depths where duckweed drifts, A bullfrog guards his watery gifts. No border drawn in ink or sand— Just this log, and this stretch of land! "Hark!" he booms with throaty cheer, While gnats and moths all quake to hear, "No foe shall breach these sacred bogs— I am the swamp's most loyal frog!" His flag? A cattail, frayed and tall, His anthem? Spring peepers' call. No taxes paid, no gold amassed, Just fierce defense of breakfast passed. He dueled a snake (and lost one toe), Chased off a rat (then ate his foe). No palace decked his humble reign— Just sunset light on lily-pane. So toast the unsung, green and proud, Who love their patch of earth out loud. Some serve with drums and bright parade, Some serve with ribbits in the shade!
