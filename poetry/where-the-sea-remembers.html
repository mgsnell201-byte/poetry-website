Where the Sea Remembers

The moon calls, and the sea rushes forward, pressing itself against the shore, warm, alive, unafraid. That’s us when it’s good— you reach for me, I spill toward you, everything certain, everything close. But then you turn, a shadow crossing your face, and the tide pulls back, leaving the sand cold, exposed. I stand there, watching you recede, wondering if you still feel it, if we’re still us. The night stretches, and I look up— stars flicker through the dark, small, steady breaths of light. They whisper to me, soft as a promise, that you’re not gone, that this quiet won’t last. The moon flares, its pull a fierce command, and the sea roars back, crashing hard against the shore. I feel you— your hands, your heat, tearing through the distance, and I’m alive again, drowning in you, my heart thundering, whole, wild, yours.
