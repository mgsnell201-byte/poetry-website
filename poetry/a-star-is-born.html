A Star Is Born

The heart that stopped, eyes gently closed, Mark when a precious life must go, Its final breath, so soft, disposed— Yet still the spirit starts to glow. No grave can hold what souls contain, Just flesh returns to earth again, While essence rises, breaks night's seam— A newborn star fulfills its dream. Through cosmic void it finds its place, A beacon born of love and grace, Each life that ends gives birth above To stellar fire, eternal love. So when you whisper, "Where are you?" Look skyward—there's your answer true: That twinkling light, so bright, so free, Is how they shine eternally. Then sing it loud, let grief depart— "Twinkle, twinkle, precious star, Spirit bright, you are not far, Love and grace shine from on high, Dancing in the starlit sky!"
