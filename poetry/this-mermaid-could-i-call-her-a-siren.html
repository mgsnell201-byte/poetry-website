This Mermaid—Could I Call Her a Siren?

I'm told that a mermaid is sweet and benign-ish, While a siren will lure you to death with her siren-ish. But honestly, what's the distinction between the two species-ical? They both have tails and make sailors act quite unreas-ical. A mermaid sits brushing her hair looking pretty and vain, A siren sits singing her song of doom and of pain. But if I saw either one while sailing the seven seas-io, I'd probably drown just the same, whether mermaid or siren-io. So call her whatever you like—the difference is merely semantic-al, When you're sinking beneath the waves, drowning is pretty un-romantic-al. The moral: whether siren or mermaid, the ending's identic-al— You're dead either way, so the labels are just rather pedantic-al.
