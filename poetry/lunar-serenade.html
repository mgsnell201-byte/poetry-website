Lunar Serenade

Midnight's hush, where shadows play, I lift my bow, begin to sway. The moon ascends, my only queen, Her light, my stage's silver sheen. In solitude, my song unfurls... The violin's voice softly swirls; D minor's softness, just for you, Rising clear against the blue. G7's tender notes arise, My offering to midnight skies. Em7's melancholy plea Between the moon and only me. F# and E, I draw them near, A secret only you can hear; Ab and Gb whisper high— A private concert in the sky. The music builds with quiet grace In this sacred, starlit space. B and A, my bow takes flight; G and F#, yours tonight. In D major's triumph clear, No other audience draws near. F# and A, and C# and E— This serenade for you, from me. My violin sings pure and true; Each note a gift, my moon, for you. Em7's yearning fills the air A duet we two now share. And when the music fades to black, A7’s sigh resonates back; The moon's sweet answer from above Blessing this nocturnal love. In silence, strings grow still; The last notes floating on night's chill. F and E, a soft goodbye, Until tomorrow's lullaby.
