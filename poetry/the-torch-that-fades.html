The Torch That Fades

In the dying embers of a fading light, Democracy's last breath whispers through the night, A once-vibrant flame, now flickers out of sight, Leaving behind a legacy of freedom's fight. The halls of power, where voices used to rise, Now echo with the silence of compromised compromise, The people's voice, a muffled whisper in the noise, As truth is twisted, and lies are made the choice. The Congress, once a beacon of hope and pride, Now crumbles under the weight of special interests' tide, The promises of change, the hopes of a brighter day, All lost in the haze of money's corrupting sway. The death of democracy, a mournful tolling bell, Echoes through the chambers of a hollowed shell, The people's trust, a bond that's broken and worn, Leaves only tears and sorrow, and a future unborn. But even as the darkness closes in, A spark remains, a glimmer of hope within, A testament to all that's been and will be, A memory of freedom's call, that still whispered "be". Let this requiem for democracy's passing be, A reminder of the power of freedom's legacy, Let us arise from the ashes of a dying dream, And rebuild the foundations of a freer future, once again.
