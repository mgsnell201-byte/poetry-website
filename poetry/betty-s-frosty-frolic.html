Betty's Frosty Frolic

From thirties' screens she sprang to life, A cartoon gal with sassy spice, Did you know she started out as a pup, Before evolving to a flapper all grown up? Betty Boop is dressing up, For the Frost Queen Ball tonight, In a gown of snowy fluff, Sparkling like the stars so bright. She winks and sings with gleeful cheer, "I wanna be loved by you!" so dear, But Frost Queen's rules are stiff and cold, Betty rebels with spirit bold. "Boop-Oop-a-Doop!" she cries with glee, Dancing wild, defying decree, The queen's stern frown turns to a grin, As Betty's fun lets laughter win!
