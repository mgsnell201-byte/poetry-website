A Minnow-t to Spare

A bait shop sign hung crooked and bright: "OUT TO LAUNCH," in paint gone thin and pale, And just below it, dozing in the light, A fisherman sat lost in some quiet tale. I thought of every sleeper who's been shown A mustache, penciled while they lay unknowing, And felt that same old mischief, fully grown, A little itch of trouble, softly growing. Slumped in his chair, his hat pulled low and deep, His breath gone slow beneath that weathered brow; I drew a mustache while he lay asleep, Then taped my little sign beneath him now. "Be Back in a Minnow-t," it proudly read, Below the dreamer, still as any stone; While "Out to Launch" hung crooked overhead, Two silly signs made mischief of their own.
