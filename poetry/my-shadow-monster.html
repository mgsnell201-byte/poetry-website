My Shadow Monster

There's a thing that comes creeping wherever I go, it follows my feet on the ground! It stretches and squishes and puts on a show, without making one single sound! I jumped on it! Stomped it! It didn't say ow! I stood on its slippery head! It just spread out flatter — I don't know quite how — and it still didn't fall over dead! I ran from it! Sprinted! It came right along, it stuck to my heels like glue! That dark squishy copycat, sneaky and strong, did every last thing that I do! Then I waved my right hand and it waved its right hand, and I waved my left — so did it! And I figured it out — it was perfectly planned — it's just ME! And I have to admit: Having two of me — one in the air, one below — is just about perfectly great! I'm incredibly handsome from both sides, you know, and my shadow thinks I'm really EIGHT!
