What Fills the Silence?

In silence where no breath disturbs the air, No morning coffee steams beside the bed, No gentle hand to brush away despair, No whispered words where hope might once have fed. The letters never written, thoughts unspoken, The garden left to wither in the rain, The promises that time has left unbroken Because there is no soul to feel the pain. No laughter echoing through empty halls, No tears to wash the dust from windowsills, No voice to answer when the darkness calls, No heart to climb tomorrow's waiting hills. The books remain unopened on the shelf, Their stories trapped in bindings, sealed and still, No eyes to read, no curious, searching self To wonder what lies past the distant hill. No hand to hold another's in the night, No shoulder offered when the world grows cold, No witness to the fading of the light, No keeper of the stories left untold. Yet in this void where nothing breathes or dreams, Perhaps the question’s yours: what breaks this night? What light do you bring to make the darkness bright?
