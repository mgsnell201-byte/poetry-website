Bean-Fueled Dreams

Old Wilbur found a farmer’s stash—
A tin of beans (the "Explosive" batch).
He scarfed them down with glee, then WHOOMP—
His belly blew him off his rump!

He zoomed up past the farmer’s shed,
A porky comet—bean-propelled red.
"I’M FLYING!" oinked the gassy hog—
Then froze. "Uh-oh. Was that a… fog?"

The barnyard watched in mixed delight
As Wilbur fart-steered left, then right…
Till one last toot—a dying squeak—
He landed thud… in Farmer’s creek.

Now Wilbur’s banned from legumes (sigh),
But winks: *"Next time? I’m trying rye.
And maybe jet packs." (Lesson learned:
All flights need thrust—not beans returned.)
