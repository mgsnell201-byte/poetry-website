The Forest Zână

I met a princess in the wood,
barefoot where the tall trees stood.
Her eyes so wide with won-der-ment,
I paused to see where magic went.

She cupped an orb of swirl-ing light—
a galaxy of color bright.
"I'm vrăjitoare of this place,"
she whispered with a flowered grace.

The forest hummed a tune so old,
her layered skirts of green and gold
swished 'round her as the light beams played.
A zână born, not made, but made!

She offered me that glowing sphere—
"It holds the seasons of the year,
the whispers of Carpathian trees,
and all the ancient mysteries."

I watched her dance on moss and stone,
this princess-witch who called woods home.
Then off she twirled through dappled shine—
a magic moment, briefly mine.
