Ram-A-Lamb-A-Ding-Dong

In March, I saw a lamb so sweet,
With horns that made my heart skip a beat—
But wait, oh no, it’s not a lamb,
It’s a goat in a scam, pulling a sham!

I called it "Lambchop," soft and mild,
But those horns poked me—oh, so wild!
It bleated back, "I’m no soft fluff,
I’m a mountain king, so call my bluff!"

The fields all laughed at my mistake,
This "lamb" charged forth, for goodness’ sake!
With prancing hooves and a cheeky grin,
It’s a ram-a-lamb—a goofy win!

So here’s to March’s woolly surprise,
A horned "lamb" with mischievous eyes—
Next time I’ll look before I leap,
Or get butted by this woolly sneak!
