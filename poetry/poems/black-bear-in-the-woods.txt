Black Bear in the Woods

Amidst the forest, a silhouette appears;

An ebony form with a gaze that peers.

A black bear stands, on its hind legs upright,

A symbol of sorrow, in the fading light.



One paw grips a branch in dying light,

Like memories fading into night.

Its eyes, a deep well of longing and pain,

Searching for something that cannot be gained.



Its fur, a dark shroud that shivers in the breeze,

Through rustling leaves, its spirit flees.

It stands so still, like a statue of old,

A monument to tales yet untold.



As the sun dips low, and the shadows grow long,

The bear's gaze lingers where love and loss belong.

A poignant reminder of the fragility of life,

A black bear's forlorn gaze etched in the fading light.
