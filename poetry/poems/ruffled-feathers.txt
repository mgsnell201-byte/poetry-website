Ruffled Feathers

I hold my morning brew tight, I say, I say,
This rooster’s angrier than a hen in a storm.
My red comb flares, steam rises like a train’s scream,
Glasses on, I squint at the world with a scowl.

Feathers ruffled, colors burst—red, green, gold—
Like a parade that took a wrong turn, boy.
I strut with a coffee cup in my wing’s grip,
Sippin’ slow, plottin’ against that noisy dawn.

That ol’ sun thinks it’s the king, I say, I say,
Wakin’ me up with its golden, loud fuss.
But I’m a bird with sass, grit, and style,
Foghorn fury in every cluck and crow I make.

Step back, you pesky chicks, give me room,
My brew’s too hot for your nonsense today.
This rooster’s got plans, got fire, got flair,
So watch out, world—I’m rulin’ this mornin’!
