When the Moon Whispers to the Tide

The sun dissolves, its honeyed embers spent,

As moonlight stitches silver through the dusk.

You stand where shadows weave their fraying tent,

Where day and night entwine in fleeting trust.



The moon ascends, a lantern carved from frost,

While waves unfurl their scrolls of salted lore.

Does she beckon with a voice half-lost,

Or do they climb to kiss her glowing core?



The ocean hums a hymn of shattered glass,

Each crest a fleeting prayer to starlit skies.

What secrets thread through currents’ ceaseless dance,

That you might catch if you could read their sighs?



Perhaps she spins her tales of cosmic drift,

Of orbiting through voids where dreams decay.

Perhaps the tide murmurs its restless gift—

A love that carves its name in spray.



The foam, a lace of whispered promises,

Curls back, then surges with a lover’s plea.

Each wave a parchment etched with lunar kisses,

Rewritten nightly by the restless sea.



You trace the moon’s pale ribbon, spun and frail,

A trembling bridge where light and water weave.

Their dialogue unfolds—a ghostly veil,

Where truth hums soft in riddles shells believe.



What binds the tide’s wild pulse to lunar flame?

What vows are sworn where crest and glow align?

They whisper while we dream, their ancient game,

In tongues of tide that spark the shore’s design.
