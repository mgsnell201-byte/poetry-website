Unveiling Truth

In the mirror, I saw a stranger, shrouded in layers of doubt,

A silhouette wrapped in expectation, of a life to figure out.

I wore masks like a second skin, painted smiles on their face,

Each flaw magnified, each whisper of truth, buried deep within my place.



I thought I was the whispers of others, their voices echoing in glee,

“Be this or that,” they’d insist, “Conform and you will see.”

Caught in the tangle of their dreams, I wandered, lost within the night,

Chasing shadows of who I should be, blind to my own light.



Life’s mirror cracked open one day, revealing a glimmer of grace,

A moment of stillness, like dawn breaking, in this bustling race.

I began to peel back the layers, the dust of doubts that had grown,

With each heartbeat, unraveling lies, rediscovering my own.



I wandered through memories, both painful and sweet,

Finding strength in the battles I fought, the places where I’d meet

The rawness of my being, unrefined but oh so real,

And the love in my laughter, the power in how I could feel.



With every step on this journey, I shed the weight of the past,

Learning that my worth isn’t measured by the shadows that I cast.

I realized I’m not the puppet of whispers or fleeting praise,

But a tapestry of moments, woven through time’s mysterious maze.



I found clarity in the quiet, in the poems that filled my soul,

In the acceptance of my journey, I learned to be whole.

The scars tell stories of resilience, of growing skin deep,

And the dreamhouse I was building was rooted in my sleep.



Now, I stand in the light, no longer clouded by fear,

A canvas of colors alive, embracing each inch near.

For I’m not just a shadow or a mask that I once wore,

I’m a spirit unbound and magnificently so much more.



In the echo of my laughter, in the strength of my embrace,

I found the beauty of my essence, the truth of my place.

No longer defined by illusions, or what others believe,

In the journey of becoming me, I’ve learned to truly see.
