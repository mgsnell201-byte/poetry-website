A Bittersweet Scent

The scent of Thanksgiving's warm delight
Swirls through the streets, filling my lonely night.
I sniff and snuff, trying to hold on tight
To memories that dance, like sparks in the light.

The aroma of roasted turkey, sage and thyme
Reminds me of family gatherings, at Thanksgiving time.
When rosemary whispered through rooms divine
And laughter echoed, like sweet mulled wine.

I remember Mom's cooking, a banquet so bright
Onions sizzling, garlic's pungent might.
My siblings and I, noses twitching with glee
Inhaling deeply the scents of our family tree.

But now, these aromas brings tears to my eyes
For all that's been lost, times bittersweet prize.
I think of Dad's cologne, as he carved with flair
And Mom's perfume, when I was feeling bare.

The memories come flooding back, in a sensory wave
Of butter-basted bird, and gravy I'd crave.
But with each whiff, my heart begins to pine
For the life I once had, now lost to time.

I think of Grandma's kitchen, her apron string-tied
"Gratitude is key," she would say with pride.
Her eyes would sparkle, like cloves in the night
As she shared her blessings, a fragrant delight.

The scent of roasting veggies, carrots caramelized
Reminds me of my own kid's joy, now realized.
There giggles echoing, as they sniffed with care
But now they're grown, leaving my house bare.

So I'll hold on tight to these olfactory cues
Let turkey and stuffing bring comforting news.
For though it brings pain, it also makes me whole
A potpourri of memories, to soothe my soul.

As I take a deep breath, savor each note
Of cranberry tartness and gravy in a boat.
I know that even in sorrow, there's still time to heal
Through the scents of Thanksgiving, emotions reveal.
