So It Goes with Frosting

Dohanos watched him, standing close,
The flour, the sweat, the practiced dose,
A man who piped what war could not—
The beautiful, the calm, the got.

Gus Volkening's hands made a ring
Of ivory sugar, offering
What governments forgot to give:
A reason, sweet and white, to live.

Pvt. Stall wore olive drab,
The color of a world gone mad,
They handed him this frosted all—
Nice, nice. So very nice, Pvt. Stall.

June '45. The guns went mute.
The ovens hummed their warm salute.
In Westport, Connecticut, the support
Was sugar. And it was. Of a sort.
