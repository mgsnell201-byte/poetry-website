Swamp Strings

In the murky depths of a "Deliverance" swamp,
an alligator sits, banjo cradled in scaly arms.
His weathered overalls sag, patched with tales of mud,
a rocking chair sways like a lazy current under him.
"Dueling Banjos" drifts through the humid air,
a grin spreads wide, teeth glinting like jagged stars.

His tail curls, a slow ripple of green muscle,
little gator kin splash around, eager apprentices.
They bob in the shallow water, eyes wide with mischief,
mimicking his strums with clumsy, webbed hands.
A blade of grass dangles from his snout, a cigar of the wild,
humor bubbles up, a croak that echoes like a chuckle.

The night wraps around, a blanket of shadows and reeds,
he plays on, a swamp maestro with no need for rhythm.
Each note a splash, each pause a breath of the bayou,
a comical king reigning over this watery stage.
