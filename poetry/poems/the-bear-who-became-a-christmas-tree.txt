The Bear Who Became a Christmas Tree

Old Bruno slumbered through winter's call,

His fur grown thick, his body tall.

As snowflakes kissed his sleepy nose,

A magic spell around him rose.



His heavy paws became a single root,

And his fur transformed into a camouflage suit

His massive arms stretched wide and high,

Reaching branches toward the sky.



Where once his heart beat warm and slow,

Now twinkled lights in cheerful glow.

His gentle spirit, kind and true,

Drew families near as Christmas grew.



Children hung their wishes bright

Upon his arms with pure delight.

He sheltered gifts beneath his frame,

Though none would ever know his name.



Bruno stood through winter's night,

A guardian bathed in golden light.

His sacrifice of flesh and bone

Made every house feel like a home.



When spring returned with warmer days,

He'd dream of summer's honey rays,

Content to know his greatest gift

Was helping weary spirits lift.
