Whispers in the Globe

In the hush of glass, suspended high,
A prisoner of breath, with muffled sigh,
Built his world, a fragile shell,
Where whispers weave a fearsome spell.

No sails unfurled, no oceans crossed,
No love's embrace, no battle lost.
He spun a web, of chilling light,
To shroud his heart from endless night.

A distant song, a melody unheard,
Where laughter blooms, he finds no word.
Their sunlit dance, a mocking flame,
As shadows coil, and whisper his name.

Ironclad fear, a chilling vice,
Holds him captive, pays a silent price.
He huddles close, a trembling form,
Dreading the storm, both cold and warm.

Frost-kissed domain, where hope grows bleak,
Can embers stir, a promise speak?
No sunlit hand, no joyous chime,
Can shatter walls, and claim his time.

The wind's soft song, a whisper near,
He feels the glass, the shifting fear.
Will he stay bound, a captive still,
Or face the fire, and climb the hill?

The fragile globe, on gossamer thread,
Hangs poised between the light and dread.
His trembling hand, a whispered plea,
Will he dare fly, or stay the sea?
