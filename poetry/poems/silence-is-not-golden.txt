Silence is Not Golden

Silence is a luxury we can't afford
In a world where our voices are reduced to a whisper
Where the loudest ones get attention, while the quiet ones are ignored
Where the noise of the system drowns out the whispers of our souls

We're taught to be still, to sit quietly, to observe and listen
But what about those who can't be still? Who are constantly on edge?
Who have been silenced too many times, who have lost their voice?

The pain in someone's eyes is a weight they carry alone
A desperation that comes with being a whisper in the wind

The quiet soul is battered by the noise of the world
But even in silence, there is strength

In holding space for those who've been silenced, we can create a new sound
A sound that's gentle, but unrelenting; a sound that's loud enough to be heard
