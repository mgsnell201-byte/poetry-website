The Great Wall of  Mauerbauertraurigkeit

Brick by brick, a wall begins to rise,
Mauerbauertraurigkeit takes hold.
Behind each stone, a heart's demise,
A story of connection untold.

The builder's hands are rough with toil,
Yet softer than the hardened heart.
This barrier, born of inner turmoil,
Keeps the world and soul apart.

Mauerbauertraurigkeit, so deep,
A sadness pushing others away.
The wall grows high, emotions steep,
In isolation, the builder stays.

But walls can crumble, bricks can fall,
When one decides to break the spell.
Mauerbauertraurigkeit, not all —
There's joy in tearing down this hell.
