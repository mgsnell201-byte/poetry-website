Winter's Quiet Song

The sky is a sheet of faded gray,
stretching thin over frosted fields—
a hush so deep, the earth forgets
to whisper back.

Bare branches etch their tangled prayers
into the wind’s cold, restless breath,
while somewhere, hidden under snow,
a seed dreams green.

Footsteps crunch the brittle dawn,
each one a fleeting signature
on time’s unblinking page—
soon erased.

The hearth hums low, a drowsy chant,
as shadows stretch and yawn,
swallowing the last thin light
of the year’s tired eye.

Night arrives with silver stars,
stitching silence to the land—
a quilt of dark and diamond bright,
winter’s lullaby.
