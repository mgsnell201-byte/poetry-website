The Witchfool's Brew

The witches found their recipe book,
"New England Toadstool Soup!" they cheered,
they didn't give the page a look—
just read it upside down (how weird!).

The first witch cried, "ʇʍǝu ɟo ǝʎǝ!"
and plopped in eyes—KERPLUNK! KERSPLAT!
The soup turned green and winked with glee,
the eyeballs blinked and stared back flat!

The second squinted, "ǝoʇǝlʇsᴉɯ!"
She dumped in toes, all gray and thick,
the cauldron burped and overflowed
with gooey, oozy, goopy sick!

The third witch read "sƃuᴉʍ ʎɹᴉɐɟ!"
and tossed in wings that flapped around,
the pot exploded purple high—
and chicken feathers hit the ground!

They flipped the book, then screamed, "OH MY!
We've been reading upside down!"
They looked down at their soup and sighed—
the health inspector's coming to town.
