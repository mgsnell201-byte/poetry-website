The Light that Stays

I saw it once, in the crack under the door,
a sliver of yellow pushing through the dark.
Not loud, not fireworks—just enough
to make me wait, to make me listen.

Some days, it’s the creak of the floorboards
at dusk, or the murmur of rain against the window.
You don’t chase it, not really—
it finds you when you forget to look.

I think it’s always there,
like the breath you don’t notice
until it steadies. Believe in that,
and the world cracks open, quiet as a seed.

So I’ll hold to the small things,
the creak, the murmur, the steady breath—
they remind me: you’re never lost,
and magic lingers for those who see.
