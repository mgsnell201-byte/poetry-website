Looking Up 'Collective

I was sitting at my desk this morning,
my coffee going cold in its mug,
when I decided to look up the word
for a group of sharks and jellyfish.

A smack, it turns out.
A smack of jellyfish.

A shiver of sharks—
now that one I understand,
the cold fear running down your spine
when you see that fin
cutting the water.

I imagine the medieval scholar
who compiled these,
perhaps a little drunk on mead,
looking out his window
at the world’s beautiful strangeness
and thinking, "Yes, that’s exactly what they are—"
a shrewdness of apes,
as if they’re all sitting around
in tweed jackets, discussing philosophy.  ,
a prickle of porcupines,
an unkindness of ravens.

And here I am, centuries later,
my coffee now completely cold,
realizing that language
is just our way of trying
to organize the chaos,
of giving names to the mystery
of things moving together.
