Burns' Hidden Flame

Where sun-kissed ewes roam heather's lilac waves,

Burns croons of barley, whispers love it craves.

His heart, a stag scarred by a feathered sting,

Hunts solace in companionship's soft spring.



"Lass wi' eyes that dance like flames' hot breath," he sighs,

"Let embers of friendship in my spirit rise.

Past fires scorched trust to ash, a barren field,

Only fellowship's balm my wounds can yield."



Torn 'twixt yearning's sigh and passion's searing kiss,

He yearns for solace, shuns love's fierce abyss.

Friendship's mantle, draped on pain's bare face,

Conceals a smoldering ember's tender grace.



So raise a toast, let bagpipes wail and soar,

His songs, a tapestry of sun and winter's roar.

For in that guise, a hidden ember glows,

A whispering secret twilight only knows.
