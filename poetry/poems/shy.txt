Shy

You say you've heard my every thought,

Like pages you've already caught.

But books don't speak their deepest part -

Some stories live inside the heart.



You want my voice to fill the air,

To chase the echoes everywhere.

But quiet isn't missing words—

It's where the truest thoughts are heard.



So name me shy, if you must—

But soil isn't less than dust.

What grows in silence stands alone,

Its roots the strength no eye has shown.
