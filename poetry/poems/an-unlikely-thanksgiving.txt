An Unlikely Thanksgiving

In twilight's hush, where shadows sweep,
Through forests ancient, dark and deep,
The autumn leaves dance wild and free,
A golden waltz for all to see,

Upon this earthen stage unfurled,
Where foxes' laughter lights their world,
Their tales, like silk threads finely spun,
Beneath the watchful stars are run,

Amidst the feast, a tiny guest
Sits perched upon a porcelain crest,
Her whiskers twitch as tales unfold,
While foxes share their stories bold,

If predator and prey can dine
In peace beneath the day's decline,
Then surely we, with hearts more wise,
Can bridge the chasms that divide.
