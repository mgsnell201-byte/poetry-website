Wearing the Echo

I wore my name like borrowed thread—
It fit but didn't cling.
I spoke in tones I thought were mine
But didn't feel them sing.

The days were clocks I watched them tick,
Each hour a quiet room.
I filled them up with practiced steps
And left before they bloomed.

Then you arrived—not loud, but sure,
A knock I didn't plan.
You asked me why the curtains closed
And touched the light I ran.

You didn't change the way I spoke
But taught me how to hear
The echo I mistook for truth
Was silence dressed in fear.

I do not mourn the me I was—
She did the best she knew.
But now I wear my name with ease
And walk in colors true.

You were not map—you were the road,
Not mirror but the face.
And I, who thought I knew the way,
Now travel into grace.
