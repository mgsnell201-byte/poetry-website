Will You Answer?

verse 1
High upon a concrete ledge,
A man stands balanced on the edge.
Below, a hundred phones held high,
Live-streaming someone's elses goodbye.
Someone shouts "Just jump, be done!
And laughter spreads through everyone.
He had a name. He had a son.
He had a war he never won.

verse 2
Pink slip Tuesday, locks were changed,
His medication rearranged.
He called the hotline, no one came.
Tomorrow he will be clicks and claims.
We watched it live, we shared the clip,
We typed our prayers, then let it slip
Into the scroll, the endless feed,
Another life we didn't need.

chorus
Will you answer when the call comes?
Will you stand when others run?
Or just turn away from the setting sun?
Will you answer? Will anyone?

verse 3
A boy of seventeen lies still
On concrete cold beneath the hill.
His mother running, slippers bare,
Screams of sirens in the air.\n
She holds his face, she begs his name,
But nothing but the echoes came.
They march on Sunday, light a flame,
By Wednesday there's another name.

verse 4
Another mother, another son,
Another hashtag, another gun.
The preachers say God has a plan,
But God ain't stopping bullets, man.

instrumental - harmonica solo, guitar picking intensifies

verse 5
On a road of dust and steel,
A young man drops, too numb to feel.
A photograph against his chest
His baby girl, three months at best.
Eight thousand miles from her first breath,
He trades his last one in for death.

verse 6
A senator will clear his throat
And read some words that someone wrote.
"A hero's sacrifice," he will say,
And vote for war again the next day.
By Monday no one knows the name
Beneath the flag, beneath the flame.
We stand, we clap, we fly the flag,
We slap a ribbon on the bag.
We say support, but what we mean
Is out of sight and off my screen.

bridge
You saw the post, you kept on scrolling,
You heard the cry, you kept on strolling,
You watched the news between your bites
Of comfort food on comfort nights.
The mirror cracks, the truth bleeds through —
The somebody was always you.

chorus
Will you answer when the call comes?
Will you stand when others run?
Or just turn away from the setting sun?
Will you answer? Will anyone?\n\n

outro - guitar becomes sparse, voice whispers
So, will you answer?
When the lights go dark and the world won't see,
Will you answer?
Or keep dancing through the tragedy.
The ledge, the war, the blood, the floor —
Can't close your eyes and dance no more.
Will you answer?
