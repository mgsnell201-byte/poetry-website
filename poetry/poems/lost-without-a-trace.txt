Lost Without a Trace

His memory deceives him, his recollection gone.
He wakes in the shadows, though it’s truly dawn.
A women's soft voice asks, “How are you today?”
But he doesn’t know her, or what to say.

A thought sparks suddenly, from the fog in his mind.
He thinks, “I know this woman, and why she is so kind.”
But like a bullet, fleeting, ever so fast,
The thought dissolves, gone without a past.

He can no longer join his waning life,
He can't remember is family and wife.
He dwells in a house unfamiliar to him,
Without hope of ever belonging again.
