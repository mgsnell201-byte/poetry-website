Santa's Audit

I want you to imagine Santa Claus
perched on the roof of Henderson & Associates Tax Services,
his sleigh tilted across the shingles,
and in his gloved hand—not his famous list—
but a roll of toilet paper.

One of the reindeer, let's say it's Prancer,
has positioned himself on the chimney top,
hunched and focused with that look of concentration
animals get when nature calls at inconvenient moments,
and you begin to understand this isn't a social visit.

Apparently, even flying reindeer need a facilities break
after half a billion houses,
and what better place than above the office
that's been sending Santa certified letters for years
about his "questionable tax status"
and "failure to report livestock depreciation."

So here's the perfect marriage of necessity and revenge—
the chimney that usually receives gifts
receiving instead a different kind of delivery.
