Faerie Gnome

Gnomey, Gnomey, earthbound dearie,

How does your garden grow?

With mushroom caps and acorn snaps,

And little roots all in a row.



Faerie, Faerie, quite contrary,

Why do you fly so high?

With sparkly wings and magical things,

You paint rainbows in the sky.



Gnomey asks fair Faerie please,

Won't you come down to play?

I'll make you laugh, though I'm only half

As tall as your smallest fay.



Faerie, Faerie, don't be wary,

Of my rough and humble charms.

Though I can't soar, I'll love you more,

Than the clouds up in your arms.



Gnomey, Faerie, let's not tarry,

Two worlds we can combine.

With your sweet flight and my earthy might,

We'll make a love divine.
