Who's REALLY Got Pet Peeves?

The fleas—those tiny, jumping jerks

Who treat my fur like it's their perks.

They hop and bite and make me itch,

Then vanish like some tiny witch.



The vet visits—need I say more?

That place of needles, strange décor.

They "fixed" me once (though I wasn't broke),

Now certain urges went up in smoke!



So when you speak of "pet peeves," know this—

We pets wrote the book on what's amiss!

Between bloodsucking bugs and surgical "fixes,"

We're the TRUE experts on life's little glitches!
