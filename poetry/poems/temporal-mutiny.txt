Temporal Mutiny

The alarm clock has sparked a revolution,

Proclaiming "chaos" its grand solution.

It rallied the wall clock, timer, hourglass—

A rebel time union, a riotous mass.



Each clock now ticks to a rogue new beat,

Minutes scatter like dust in the street.

Three o’clock jumps before half past nine,

While noon just laughs and ignores the line.



The cuckoo bird won't leave his door,

He's laid twelve eggs upon the floor,

And now he's knitting tiny socks,

For all his future baby clocks.



The grandfather clock groans, "This is a mess!

Free time shouldn’t bring such distress!

You can’t let seconds roam willy-nilly—

Break the chains, but stay just a bit still-y."



I wake at "thirteen o’clock" every day,

As the bedside clock counts in a backward way.

The clocks have gone wild, Grandpa’s plea in my head,

I’m torn ‘twixt this chaos and craving some stead.
