The Day You Escaped

Congratulations on surviving another lap
Around the sun without a major mishap!
Your parents probably thought "Oh no, what have we done?"
But here you are—their work is never done!

Your knees now crack like bubble wrap,
You grunt when rising from a nap,
Your back goes out more than you do,
But hey—you're still breathing, so woo-hoo!

Another year of questionable choices,
Of listening to your body's creaky voices,
Of pretending you remember people's names,
And losing constantly at memory games!

So raise a glass (but not too high—your shoulder!)
To getting magnificently older,
May your joints stay somewhat functional,
And your bladder remain punctual!
