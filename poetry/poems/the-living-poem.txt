The Living Poem

Underneath a sapphire sky,
Where the swift birds freely roam.
Beyond thick leaves, with cicadas' sighs-walls,
There lies our verdant home.

Breezy whispers waltz around,
Through trees dressed in emerald grain.
The sun parades its golden crown
And paints life over plain.

Daylight yawns and moon awakes,
Crickets hum their twilight song.
With tales whispered by ancient lakes,
Awash stars where dreams belong.

Observe dear Earth’s eternal reel
Unbound is nature's humble tome:
In serene silence we unveil
The beauty of her living poem.
