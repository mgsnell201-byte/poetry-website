The Whisper of the Pines

The forest hums with secrets none can name,
Its pine trees sway beneath a silver sky.
Their needles brush the air with soft lament,
As if to speak of lives that linger here.
The path winds slow through shadows deep and still,
Where moss clings low to granite’s weathered face.
Each step I take disturbs the quiet earth,
Yet nature holds no grudge for human tread.

The brook nearby runs cold with mountain snow,
Its murmur weaves a thread through ancient stone.
I pause to watch the light on water dance,
A fleeting gift before the dusk descends.
The trees stand guard, their roots entwined with time,
Each ring a tale of winters overcome.
What man could know the weight of seasons borne,
When all his days are but a fleeting breath?

The wind grows sharp, it cuts through woolen coat,
Yet warmth persists in how the land endures.
The field beyond lies open to the stars,
Its grasses bow to night’s unyielding call.
I stand and listen to the pines’ low chant,
Their voices blend with dreams of those long gone.
This land outlives our striving and our pain,
Its quiet heart beats on where ours may fade.
