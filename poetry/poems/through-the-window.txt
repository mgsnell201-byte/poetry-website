Through the Window

I bare the curtains, stop and stare,

Through morning mists that drift in air,

The scene before me starts to write,

A story with its sacred rite.



As morning's secrets come to see,

The dawn light sets my vision free,

Beyond the rows of flowers fair,

Time melts into the morning air.



Now nature paints its perfect sight,

As wisdom blooms in morning's light,

Each breath I steal helps me to heal,

As deeper truths begin to reveal.



This wooden sill, worn smooth with care,

Has held each quiet moment there,

While framing every precious scene,

That taught me what these mornings mean.



Through daily rites and morning's rays,

Each dawn presents its gifts and ways,

Here time stands still upon this sill,

Where heart and spirit drink their fill.
