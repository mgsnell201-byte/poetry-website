Does Two Plus Two Equal Four?

My teacher says that two plus two
Will always equal four,
But I say that's ridiculous—
It could be three, or more!

For instance, if you've got two cats
And then you get two more,
You might end up with five or six
(If someone's keeping score).

Because those cats might have some kittens
Hiding in a drawer,
So two plus two cats doesn't equal
Four cats anymore!

And if you've got two scoops of ice cream,
Then you get two more,
By the time you eat them all
There's zero on your floor!

My teacher frowned and shook her head
And pointed to the door,
But I still say that two plus two
Might not be always four.
