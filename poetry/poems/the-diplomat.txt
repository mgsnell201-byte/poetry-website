The Diplomat

My purpose is a brief descent.
A wait in the dim, papery dark,
then the spin, the unspooling,
the dizzying fall from the roll.
The sudden tear of separation.
Now, I am a sheet. A shield.

The Crumple.
A practiced, hurried fist transforms me
into a soft weapon, a cloud.

Then, the plunge into the damp unknown,
the terrain I was made to meet.
I am the silent diplomat,
the gentle blotter of aftermath.
The only line of defense,
absorbing what the body has dismissed.

My pristine surface, briefly white.
I am the bargain struck with mess,
the necessary, fleeting touch
that spares the hand from truth.

A single, functional moment—
to dab, to press, to make clean—
is my entire existence.

Then, the final letting go.
The whirlpool's hungry pull.
A descent into the deep, dark plumbing,
taking the evidence with me.

I am used, I am soiled, I am gone.
Another takes my place on the roll,
waiting for their own brief descent.
