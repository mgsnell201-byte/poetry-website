The Hand Remains Open (The Space Between Breaking)

the cup left the hand

and turned once in the air

slow as a leaf in still water



the floor waited

the tiles were cold and separate



in that turning

the light found its way

through the space where the whole had been



not warm

not kind

only the color of rain after it has passed



I have stood there

watching the arc complete itself

as if the breaking were a question



the hand remained open

nothing in it now

nothing to close



you were still breathing then

or the word had not yet landed

or the door had swung but not shut



the light stayed

filling the gap

making each edge sharp



as the last thing seen

before the dark knows its name



the cup lies

in its pieces

each one exact



and the silence between them

goes on listening
