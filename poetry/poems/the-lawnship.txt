The Lawnship

Ragnar the Brave, a Viking so bold,
In a cardboard helm, a sight to behold.
His companion, loyal and true,
Was Ember the drake with scales green and blue.

They sailed their lawnship from morn till night,
Through deep, rolling waters by pale moonlight.
Ember soared high on silvery wings,
Told Ragnar tales of the songs she sings.

His yarn-beard flowed, his shield shone bright,
As Ember breathed smoke in the soft dawn light.
They fought fierce monsters that lurked in the deep,
While garden gnomes trembled, too scared to peep.

But when sun set, their play would cease,
The warrior and dragon found rest and peace.
Their voyage done, adventure withdrawn,
The mighty lawnship parked safe on the lawn.

For Ragnar the Brave was just seven years old,
And Ember the Drake was his cat, truth be told.
