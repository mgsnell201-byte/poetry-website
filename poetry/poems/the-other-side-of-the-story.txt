The Other Side of the Story

The chicken crossed the road because

She heard McDonald's was hiring a new crew,

Thinking she'd beat them to the cause

And serve herself in nuggets, too.



But wait! She had a different plan—

She'd seen a dating app called "Cluck,"

Where roosters posed with protein cans

And promised her some feathered luck.



She waddled past the traffic light,

Her GPS was set to "BAWK,"

The rubber chicken squeaked in fright

As the real ones learned to jaywalk.



With dreams of work and love combined,

She thought she’d found her perfect fate.

But halfway through, she changed her mind—

KFC’s bright sign sealed her fate.



The moral here is crystal clear:

Just stick to laying eggs instead,

'Cause crossing roads brings trouble near—

You'll end up scorched and crispy-dead.
