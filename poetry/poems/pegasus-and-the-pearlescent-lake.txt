Pegasus and the Pearlescent Lake

With wings of wind-whipped moonbeams, he dances on the breeze,

Pegasus, the stardust stallion, where sky and slumber meet with ease.

His mane, a waterfall of whispers, trails a shimmering, silver wake,

As hooves of pearled quicksilver whisper secrets on the dawnlit lake.



He snorts a symphony of stardust, his eyes, twin pools of twilight's glow,

Flicker through the fields of fizzgiglets, where wishes bloom and never go.

His tail, a comet's silken banner, paints the heavens with delight,

It gilds the slumbering slumbertrees with pearlescent, moonlit light.



He gallops on the starlit meadows, a whisper on the dreamscape's hem,

A twinkle on the dream-kissed river, a lullaby for slumber's gem.

With every beat of moonlit feathers, he scatters laughter's chime,

And wakes the world with whispered secrets on wings of morning rhyme.



But hush! A glint in twilight's eye, a snicker in the breeze,

For Pegasus, the trickster prince, loves hiding in the trees.

He'll weave you dreams of spun stardust, then vanish in a wink,

Leaving starlight in his wake, and laughter on the brink.



So if you ever hear a whinny on a wind-kissed, velvet morn,

Or see a shadow dance in sunlight, on a cloud of silk reborn,

It might be Pegasus, the stardust steed, with secrets in his eyes,

A playful prince of moonlit mischief, in skies of sweet surprise..
