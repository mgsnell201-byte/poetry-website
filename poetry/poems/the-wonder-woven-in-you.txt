The Wonder Woven in You

What spark resides in you, so bright,
A glow that hums through years gone by?
Your heart, a lamp in softest light,
Weaves dreams where quiet hopes comply.

A path you tread, though worn, still gleams,
Each step a note in life’s old song.
Do you not see how courage beams,
How strength in gentle steps grows strong?

Your breath, a breeze that lifts the morn,
Ignites a flame no time can sway.
Oh, you, with wonder softly born,
You shine where memories hold their play.
