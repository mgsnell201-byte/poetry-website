The Deep Below

Everything about him was old except his eyes,
They were the color of the sea,
Where stars wheeled through darkened skies,
And mysteries ran free.

The old man rowed far beyond all ships,
Where currents dance and sway,
Past where the deep horizon dips,
Into the breaking day.

The great fish moved so beautifully,
With such dignity and grace,
Too deep beneath the restless sea,
Too far to find his trace.

Pain does not matter to a man,
He thought through salted air,
As waves against the vessel ran,
And hope mixed with despair.

The fish is my brother, he said then,
But I must kill him true—
The line cuts deep through flesh again,
As brothers sometimes do.

Man is not made for defeat, he knew,
While stars moved through the night,
Yet something in the waters blue
Would test his will to fight.

A man can be destroyed but not defeated,
Like the sea that has no end—
Time and tide forever sheeted,
Neither foe nor friend.
