Zoo-nited States of DMV

In the DMV, a waiting room zoo,
Where sloths move faster than the queue.
A chorus of coughs and sniffles deep,
As hopeful drivers in line they steep.

A toddler wails, a symphony of woe,
His mother sighs, "Just five more numbers to go!"
A bored teen taps, his foot a restless beat,
Dreaming of freedom, on gasoline-fueled feet.

An elder dozes, wisdom in his beard,
Witnessed DMV dramas, absurd and unheard.
He'll outlast them all, this wrinkled old sage,
A champion of waiting, turning page by page.

But wait! A glimmer, a window's bright plea,
Please proceed to window three!
With shuffling steps and sighs that turn to cheers,
The chosen one to freedom disappears.

Yet here I sit awaiting my plight,
Hoping to hear "you may enter the light"
