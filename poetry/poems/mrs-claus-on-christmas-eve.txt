Mrs. Claus on Christmas Eve

She settles in her favorite chair,
The sleigh bells fading in the air,
A steaming mug clasped in her hands,
While Santa flies to distant lands.

She thinks of children fast asleep,
Of promises her husband keeps,
And in her mind the presents dance—
Each toy and doll in wild romance.

The sugarplums go whirling round,
Not candies, no—but joy she's found
In knowing that by morning's light
A thousand hearts will shine so bright.

She sips her cocoa, sweet and warm,
And dreams of gifts through snow and storm,
Her own delight: to sit and see
The magic of his delivery.

The reindeer hoofprints in the snow,
The cookies nibbled—all for show—
She smiles to think what parents feel
When Christmas morning makes it real.
