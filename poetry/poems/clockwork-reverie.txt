Clockwork Reverie

The gears grind, the cogs churn,

Tempus fugit, time's tide turns.

This clockwork ursine, a wondrous sight,

Gazes back through the lens of bygone light.



A reimagining of epochs gone astray,

When steam and steel forged destiny's sway.

This mechanized bear, part beast, part machine,

Stands timeless, 'tween two worlds unseen.



The clock face o'er its vigilant eye

Marks the moments as seconds fly by.

Yet in this bruin, the past holds fast,

A fusion of then with the world that's passed.
