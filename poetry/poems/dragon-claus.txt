Dragon Claus

Old Santa Claus woke up and sneezed,
his nose was red, his throat was squeezed,
"I'm sick!" he cried, "I'm cold! Achoo!
I wish I was a dragon too."

He fell asleep and dreamed of fire,
of burning hot and climbing higher,
and when he woke, what did he see?
Green scales where his arms should be!

His beard had turned to fiery breath,
his sleigh would surely burn to death,
but Santa—now called Dragon Claus—
just grinned and didn't even pause.

He grabbed his sack with claws so bright,
and flew off on his own, that night,
delivering gifts with fiery cheer,
the bestest Santa of the year.

The kids all cheered as down he flew,
and softly landed on the ground.
Beside the tree, what met his view?
Cookies—toasted  perfectly brown!
