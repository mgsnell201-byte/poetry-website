A Season of Compromise

Autumn arrives with gentle ease,

A bridge between summer's warmth and winter's freeze.

She whispers softly to the fading light,

And brings forth winter's icy night.



Like a mother's hand on her child so dear,

Autumn guides both seasons, calm and clear.

Summer's fiery blaze she gently slows,

And brings forth autumn's soft, golden glows.



The wind that blows through autumn's leaves so bright,

Is also the one that whispers through winter's night.

But Autumn's presence brings a peaceful hue,

A season of transition, with love shining true.



So let us cherish autumn's gentle care,

The warmth it brings, and the love it shares.

For in her heart, we find a peaceful place,

Where summer's joy and winter's calm share a common space.
