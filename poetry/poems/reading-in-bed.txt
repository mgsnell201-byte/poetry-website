Reading in Bed

I have been trying to finish this novel for three weeks,
which is not a criticism of the novel
but of the way sleep ambushes me
somewhere around page twelve,
the book folding shut on my chest
like a small, patient animal
that has learned to wait.

The author, who is dead,
spent four years writing the scene
I failed to finish on Tuesday,
and again on Wednesday,
and once more last night
before the lamp clicked off
by itself, or by my hand—
I honestly can't say.

There is something I owe him,
though I'm not sure what.

Maybe just this:
the fact that his sentences
are still moving through me
somewhere below the level of sleep,
rearranging things,
the way a river
continues to work on a stone
long after you've gone home
and had dinner
and forgotten all about rivers.

I'll try again tonight.
Page twelve.
The lamp.
The stone.
