A Home Recipe

Three cups of patience, finely sifted,

Two teaspoons of care,

A tablespoon of kindness gifted

Measured with a prayer.



Add dashes of forgiveness pure,

A pound of gentle touch,

Fold laughter in to make it sure—

Never add too much.



Then bake with time at steady heat,

Season well with grace,

The recipe makes love complete

For all who find this place.
