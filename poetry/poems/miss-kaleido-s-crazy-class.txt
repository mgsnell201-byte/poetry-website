Miss Kaleido’s Crazy Class

She burst into our classroom door,

With socks that shimmered off the floor.

Her dress looked like a birthday cake,

Her glasses screamed, “I’m wide awake!”



She wrote on the board with rainbow chalk,

Made the globe spin when she'd walk.

Built a graph from sticky notes,

And turned our tests into paper boats.



Taught us math with twisty rhymes,

Mixed up numbers with the times.

Wrote sideways on the whiteboard wall,

Then danced through rows to take roll call.



Asked me why my chart was bad,

I said, “I think I saw your plaid.”

She laughed and twirled and waved goodbye—

Then vanished with a chalkboard sigh.



The bell rang loud. Her desk was bare.

Just scribbled graphs and empty air.
