Bell in the Current

You move through crowds like ancient tides,

Your thoughts as clear as morning glass,

While others rush with urgent strides,

You let the moment slowly pass.



No rigid spine distorts your form—

Instead, you bend with life's deep flow,

Through calm and wild, through peace and storm,

You pulse with rhythms few can know.



Your touch can heal or leave a sting,

Depending on the heart you meet,

Some fear the truth that you might bring,

Others find your presence sweet.



Translucent soul in murky seas,

You drift where instinct softly calls,

No anchor chains, no guarantees,

Just grace that rises, grace that falls.



The light shines through your honest core,

Revealing depths both strange and true—

A mystery the world adores,

Yet rarely understands like you.
