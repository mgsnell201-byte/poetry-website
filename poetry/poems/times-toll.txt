Times Toll

As I'm walking along the warm sandy shore,
I hear laughter, something to explore.
A short distance in front I can see,
a young girl and her dog running towards me.

With the ebb of each wave a soothing melody plays,
A rhythm she knows well as she dances with the waves.
Her smile is contagious, it brightens up the sight,
Like a comet's trail on a dark summers night.

In this peaceful scene, time stands still and slow,
While her laughter is carried on by the breeze's gentle blow.
I smile, feeling grateful in seeing this sight,
As the sun dips low, casting a warm, golden light.

A surge of emotions rise up from the deep,
Some which I've tried to forget but just had to keep.
Of times long ago when I was young and free,
Without any worries to bother me.

Sadness creeps in, a tear trickles down my cheek,
Whishing I was younger and my memories complete.
I to ran with my own dog, on this very shore,
With unbridled joy, our spirits soared.

But now, the years have taken their toll,
Time's fragile thread, begins to unravel cold.
My steps are slow, my heart beats less fast,
As I behold this scene, that will forever last.

In this moment, as the sun dips low,
I'm filled with a sense of sadness and woe.
For in my hourglass, the last grains will soon be spent,
And this sweet joy, will be but a memory to relent.
