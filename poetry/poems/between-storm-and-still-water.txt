Between Storm and Still Water

Beneath the surface, currents war—
The deep against the shallow tide,
While waves crash heavy on the shore
Of all I've tried to keep inside.

The horizon splits in two each dawn:
One half ablaze with lightning's wrath,
One half the peace I've never drawn
From walking down this chosen path.

In hurricane's eye, I stand alone,
Where silence holds the chaos back,
The waters rise, then turn to stone—
This calm that follows the attack.

What sailor learns to trust the sea
That drowns him in its darkest hour,
Yet lifts him up when he's set free
To feel its devastating power?

The storm is not my enemy—
It carves new channels in the sand,
Reshapes the coastline that is me,
Reveals what firm foundations stand.

Salt burns the wounds the tempest made,
But healing comes with morning's light,
When troubled waters slowly fade
And leave reflections, clear and bright.

I am the storm, I am the sea,
The captain and the drowning man,
Learning that to truly be
Means holding what I cannot plan.

So let the waves crash overhead,
Let thunder shake what needs to break,
For in the depths where I once fled,
I find the stillness storms can make.
