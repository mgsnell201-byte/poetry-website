Morning on the Shore

The morning breaks its cloudy veil,
The sky turns soft and bright.
The shore, a bride in dawn’s new day,
First light that clears the night.

A seal leaps through rolling waves,
Its arcs catch dawn’s first flame.
The sea’s low hum, like whispered staves,
Speaks truths without a name.

A seagull’s shrill cry splits the air,
It wheels above the foam.
The tide rolls gently, free of care,
As if it knows its home.

What pulls the heart to watch this scene?
The light that shifts and plays?
The dolphins dance where waves convene,
In morning’s golden haze.

I stand on sand, the world’s renewed,
The shore feels wide and free.
The sea’s vast pulse, so long pursued,
Says more than I can see.
