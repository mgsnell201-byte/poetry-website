Bold B

Bumbling, bouncing, beautifully bent,

B stands boldly when A is spent.

Two bubbles blown and caught mid-air,

A backbone straight with rounded flair



Brother to C, but bolder in stance,

Building words at every chance.

Bread and butter, books and bees,

B brings bounty with such ease.



Born from symbols long since gone,

B has beckoned since the dawn

Of written words and whispered tales,

Where language blooms and never fails.



Bold and bouncing, curved and bright

B brings our language into light.

From dawn of writing to this hour,

B shows its lasting, building power.
