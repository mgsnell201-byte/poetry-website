O  Closed Eye

Every year without knowing it I have

passed through a door

that opens only when the eyes

close

today I shut them

and the dark is not

dark

but a way through



A bird I cannot name

singing from a branch

I have never seen



my mother calling

from a kitchen

already

gone

the sound crosses

nothing to reach me



The grain of a table

worn by hands

I will never hold



cold stone under

bare feet in a house

with no door

back



Rain from a country

I have not

returned to

salt on the lip

of someone still breathing

in a year that has passed through me



the tongue finds

what the mind

lost



Then what has no name

among the senses

the place I go

when every door in the body

has opened



where the dead

sit in their own light

not waiting

not asking

to be found



I have been traveling toward it

all my life



the country

on the other side of the eye

where I arrive

alone

and know

everything I touch

by its

going
