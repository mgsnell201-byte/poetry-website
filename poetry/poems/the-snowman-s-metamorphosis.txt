The Snowman's Metamorphosis

In winter's chill, he stood so white,
A snowman proud, a frosty sight.
But as the warm sun graced the land,
He changed, but not as we'd planned.

His coal-black eyes began to gleam,
His stick arms stretched, or so it seemed.
His snowy form, once round and plump,
Began to shift, to move, to jump.

No puddle formed beneath his base,
Instead, he grew a human face.
His carrot nose turned flesh and bone,
A marvel that was yet unknown.

The scarf unwound, became his hair,
His buttons changed to clothes to wear.
The snowman that we built with glee,
Transformed into humanity.

So next you build a man of snow,
Remember this tale and you'll know:
Sometimes when spring melts winter's art,
A snowman might just find his heart.
