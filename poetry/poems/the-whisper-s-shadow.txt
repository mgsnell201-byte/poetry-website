The Whisper's Shadow

There's a hint inside the hint,
A suggestion's shadow cast—
"The innuendo of the innuendo"
Echoes what was never asked.

Not the wink behind the word,
Not the mask, but the echo's glide—
The afterword of implication,
The tremor it leaves inside.

It lives in doubled doubles,
The subtext beneath subtext,
Where indirectness folds itself
Into nests of what comes next.

A meta-whisper, vapor-thin,
The shape of what was never said—
"The innuendo of the innuendo"
Now haunting in its stead.

It's the art of almost-speaking,
The pause that says it all,
The meaning caught in silence
When direct words would fall.

But sometimes even whispers
Can cut too sharp and deep—
"The innuendo of the innuendo"
Helps tender truths to keep.
