Wheelie Fun with Squeaks McSqueak

Squeaks McSqueak, the guinea pig bold,
With ears like sails and fur like gold,
Found a green scooter, sleek and fun,
Left by a giant, on the morning run!

He scampered aboard, paws on the grip,
Heart like a drum, whiskers a-flip,
Legs like pistons, strong and quick,
Green machine hummed, a happy click!

Through fields of clover, past buzzing bees,
Snuffling the breeze with a sniff and a wheeze,
Squirrels chit-chatted, birds in the sky,
Squeaks McSqueak zoomed, with a twinkle in his eye!

Over a bridge, tippy-toe slow,
Past sleepy cows with a moo-moo low,
Down a dusty lane, sun on his nose,
Squeaks' big adventure, where anything goes!

Home he scurried, tales to tell,
Of whizzing wheels and a magic spell,
Green scooter tucked, in hay so dry,
Squeaks McSqueak, with a wink and a sigh.

For in his dreams, the scooter flies,
Through rainbow clouds, reaching for the skies,
A tiny hero, brave and free,
Squeaks McSqueak, and wild machinery!
