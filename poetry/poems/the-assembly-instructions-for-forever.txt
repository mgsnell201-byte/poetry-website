The Assembly Instructions for Forever

Forever arrives in a cardboard box
marked FRAGILE—yet inside, only Nows:
hundreds of them, identical as checkers,
none more important than the next.

You'd think there'd be blueprints,
some cosmic diagram showing
which Now connects to which,
but there's only a note that says:

"Some assembly required.
All parts already included."

The mad thing is, you can't save them up.
Each Now insists on being opened immediately,
used completely—then it's gone,
not broken, just transformed
into the glue that holds the others.

I tried to peek ahead once,
to see what Forever looks like finished,
but Forever's funny that way.
It only exists in the building,
one Now clicking into the next.

And if you stop to admire your work—
well, that's just another Now too,
equally forever as the rest.

So here we are: mud creatures
constructing eternity
from moments that won't keep,
and somehow—somehow—
it all holds together beautifully,
or at least it holds.
