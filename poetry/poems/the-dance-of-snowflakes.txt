The Dance of Snowflakes

The wind, a playful piper, leads the snowflakes in airy flight,

 A million tiny dancers, twirl in the morning light.

 The trees, in frosted slumber, wear their crowns of crusted white,

 Diamond branches reaching high, to catch the sun's delight.



 The world, a canvas painted fresh, in hues of silver, crisp and clean,

 A hush descends, a peaceful pauses, where winters magic gleams,

 But, don't you fret, for winter's heart, though wrapped in icy chill,

 Holds warmth within its frozen grip, a joy that lingers still.



 The crunch of boots on frosty ground, a symphony to the ears,

 The laughter rings, like windchimes sweet, dispelling winter's fears.

 A snowball fight, a frosty war, with forts of ice so grand,

 Red cheeks aglow, hearts warm and bright in winters wonderland.



 So bundle close, and let the wind, whisk away your woes,

 For winter's magic brings a charm, that on laughter shows.

 With cocoa warm in mugs held close, and stories whispered low,

 We'll chase the shadows, dance with frost, and let our spirits glow.



 For winter's not a time for tears. but one for joy to bring,

 A Season where the world's reborn, on frosty angel's wings.

 So let the snowflakes fall like stars, and paint the world anew,

 In Winters whimsy, find your joy, in dreams that sparkle true!
