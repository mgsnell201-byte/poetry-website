Unseen Echoes

If a life fades in shadows deep,

With no one there its tale to keep,

Does silence drown the spark

That kindled in the dark?



Does frost at dawn still trace

The paths no gaze will chase?

Do petals burn with unseen flame

For eyes that never came?



The stars inscribe each fleeting breath—

Each laugh, each sigh, each brush with death.

Love whispered to the empty air

Still lingers, though none were there.



The man who broke his meager bread

For sparrows, murmuring as they fed,

Died alone where rivers bend—

Yet wings salute their silent friend.



Each life casts ripples through the tide,

In waves where quiet truths abide.

A stone’s soft fall shapes seas unknown,

Its echo carved in time’s own stone.



The tree that falls sings fierce and clear,

No ear required to overhear.

A life unwatched still claims its space—

Its pulse resounds in boundless grace.



No witness needs to mark its worth,

No voice to sing its fleeting birth.

Your question lights the truth’s soft flame:

It matters—ask, and name its name.
