Autumn's Last Breath

As Nature's final dance begins to fade,
Golden leaves surrender to the ground.
Summer's warmth is lost, and Winter's shade,
Falls softly, without a sound.

The trees, like skeletal fingers, stand
Bare and forlorn, their beauty lost from sight.
Their limbs, like withered arms, outstretched hand,
Beckon Spring to bring new light.

But even in decay, there's hidden might,
For in the earth, seeds lie and wait to rise,
A promise of renewal, shining with new light,
To burst forth, like a phoenix, with a vibrant surprise.

And when Spring's warmth awakens the morning air,
The trees regain their vibrancy, and new life begins,
Their green leaves unfurl, with a vibrant flair,
And flowers bloom, surrounded by warmth and gentle winds within.
