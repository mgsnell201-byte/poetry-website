The Day I Saw What I Could See

Today I thought I saw a saw

that saw the things I couldn’t see,

it buzzed through breakfast, toast and jaw,

then calmly turned and looked at me.



“I saw you SEE,” the saw announced,

“you saw me SAW that crooked tree.”

I blinked, my eyeballs bounced and bounced—

who saw which seeing, it, or me?



I saw a sea of eyes that saw,

a see of seas that said, “We’re free!”

We saw right through my teacher’s law

of “Only see what’s there to see.”



Now every time I think I see,

I ask, “Or did I see a saw?”

The more I see, the less I’m sure—

and that’s the best sight of them all.
