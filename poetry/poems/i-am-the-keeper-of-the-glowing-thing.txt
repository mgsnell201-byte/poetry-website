I Am the Keeper of the Glowing Thing

I sit upon a mushroom throne
that hums and shivers, soft as bone—
its cap is velvet, old as trees,
it smells of grandmas and warm cheese!

My palms hold light like glowing jam,
the sweetest, brightest thing I am—
it pulses, wiggles, almost sings,
and tickles fairy-lantern wings!

My gnome-pal scrapes his squeaky bow,
each note tastes fizzy, warm, and slow—
the mouse in hat plays fiddle too,
his lullaby sounds greenish-blue!

The forest leans in close to hear,
the roots go whooshing ear to ear—
the shadows taste of mint and mud,
the beetles dance and thump and thud!

The music fades, the fairies snore
tucked in my beard (there's room for more)—
I hold the glow for roots, for night,
for anyone who needs a light.
