Fractured Horizons

In the eye of the storm, they thrash about
like a leaf on a tornado's path
their world a maelstrom of contradictions
a swirling vortex of emotions, thoughts, and desires

Their mind is a maze of twisted corridors
narrow passageways that lead to dead ends
each step forward met with uncertainty
as if the ground beneath their feet was shifting sands

The din of distractions assaults their senses
a cacophony of notifications, demands, and expectations
their attention fragmented like shattered glass
scattered across a thousand disparate surfaces

Their breath comes in sharp gasps, as if they're running
their heart racing with anticipation, anxiety, and stress
each heartbeat a countdown to the next deadline
the next meeting, the next appointment, the next obligation

And yet, amidst this turmoil, they find moments of stillness
in the quiet eye of their own gaze, where the world slows down
a fleeting sense of peace that's lost as soon as it's found
leaving them gasping for air, scrambling to regain control.
