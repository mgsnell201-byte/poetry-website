Count Elfula’s Spooky Spin

Count Elfula pops out at dusk,
His cloak’s all swirly, soft as husk.
He skips where chalk-white lanterns glow,
And winks at shadows in a row.

You mustn’t say he can’t go play,
Don’t tell him ghosts should fade away!
He tugs at clouds of crimson mist,
And hugs the moon with every twist.

What’s that? He’s munching candy corn?
His cape got tangled in a thorn!
He rolls down grass where sidewalks end,
And giggles with the midnight wind.

Oh, Elfula, keep spinning free,
Your silly dance makes stars all glee!
On Halloween, your grin’s the best,
It lights the night with goofy zest.
