The Teapot Taco

I'm a little taco chef, short and round,

Sizzling in my kitchen, flavor-bound.

When I get all fired up, I holler, "¡Qué bravura!"

“Tacos frescos!” — and one crisp flauta!



I can make you carnitas or carne asada too,

Pile on the cilantro, salsa verde so true.

When I see you're hungry, don't you pout,

Tip me over and tacos pour out!



I'm a little taco chef, yes it's true,

Making tasty treasures just for you.

With my little apron and chef hat cute,

Every taco I make is an absolute hoot!
