The Wheel of Seasons

When spring arrives with gentle hands of rain,
She wakes the earth from winter's silent sleep,
And bids the buried seeds their vigil keep
Until the sun dissolves each icy chain.
Then summer comes with gold and crimson stain,
Her bounty falls where autumn winds will reap
The harvest moon that makes the farmers weep
With joy to see such wealth from sun and rain.

But winter waits with patient, crystal breath
To blanket all beneath her silver veil,
When every leaf has danced its way to death
And bare-branched trees tell their eternal tale.
Through seasons' turn, through life and gentle death,
Love's cycle spins—and never once will fail.
