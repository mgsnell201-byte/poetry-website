When Polar Opposites Collide

The Polar Bear came trudging through

With fluffy coat and walking stick, too,

A Christmas tree upon his back—

But wait! The GRINCH began to attack!



The Grinch was POLAR opposite—

He HATED joy, he HATED it!

He grabbed the tree right off the Bear

And cackled, "Christmas? NOT THIS YEAR!"



The Bear just smiled (which made Grinch madder),

Pulled out ornaments from his pack-ladder,

And hung them on the Grinch's nose—

Bright shiny bells and silver bows!



The Grinch yelled, "STOP! You're breaking rules!

Christmas is for silly FOOLS!"

The Bear said soft, "That's where you're wrong—

Christmas isn't stuff. It's SONG."



He hummed a tune, so warm, so true,

The pine trees swayed, the snowflakes flew.

The Grinch's heart went THUMP-THUMP-CRACK—

He dropped the tree and gave it BACK.



"I guess," he mumbled, "I was mean.

You're POLAR opposite—serene."

The Bear just winked and said, "That's right.

Now help me hang these Christmas lights."
