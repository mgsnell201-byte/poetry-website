The Witch's Raven

The witch stirs cauldrons of moon-bright goo,
Concocting cloudstorms, midnight dew.
Her fingers dance like spider-webs,
Braiding spells from tidal ebbs.

The raven pecks at shiny things,
Collecting secrets, bottle rings.
His eyes are dark as magic ink—
He steals the words before you think.

Alone, she brews but can't re-call
Which pinch of star, which cricket's crawl.
Alone, he hoards but cannot speak
The shimmer-language that they seek.

Together now! She stirs, he caws!
They mix the moonlight's silver laws.
Her pot goes BOOM! His wings go CRACK!
They've spell-invented Dark-to-Back!

The stars rain down in twinkling spree,
As witch and raven laugh with me.
Their bond unbreakable, you see—
A coven born from witchery.
