Parched

I’ve stood out here for eighty years,

With dust and beetles in my ears.

The sky is blue, the sun is bright,

It’s frankly a disgusting sight.



My leaves are crisp, my twigs are brittle,

The clouds aren't helping—not a tittle!

I’m praying for a heavy pour,

To wash the squirrels from my door.



My bark is itchy, roots are dry,

I’m glare-ing at a heartless sky.

I’ve raised my limbs in desperate plea,

For some humidity for me!



My rings are tight, my sap is thin,

I want a supervisor in!

I’ll stand here stiff and gnarled and gray,

Until a storm comes through my way.
