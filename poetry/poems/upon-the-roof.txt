Upon The Roof

Upon the roof, a joyful hue,

A canvas bright, forever new,

With strokes of crimson, gold, and green—

A world imagined, soft and serene.



Laughter dances on the breeze,

Sunlight filters through the trees.

Each dripping streak, a playful trace,

Reflects a spirit’s warm embrace.



The house itself, a vibrant dream,

Beside a softly flowing stream,

A sanctuary built with care,

Beyond all sorrow or despair.



She wields her brush, a graceful hand,

Across the walls, a promised land,

Where colors mingle, soft and bright,

Chasing shadows from the light.



Her laughter echoes, sweet and clear,

Dispelling worries, calming fear.

And should you glimpse a flash so white,

A fleeting shimmer in the light,



It was not she who brought the grace,

Nor painted smiles upon her face,

But something vanished, swift and free—

A unicorn’s brief reverie.
