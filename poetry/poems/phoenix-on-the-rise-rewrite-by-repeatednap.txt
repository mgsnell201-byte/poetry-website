Phoenix on the Rise (Rewrite) by RepeatedNap

The eyes of the soulless, cold and unblinking,

Their constant and hollow gaze pins me in place.

Their mouths are dank caves, endlessly drinking

The light from the air, and speaking with grace

Of nothing that matters. I break from their stare,

And run for a door that is closing forever.

I plunge through the oaks and the choking, blind air

Where fog clings like smoke from a subterranean fire.



I run without rest, for a night and a day,

Then another, and another, ‘til time came undone.

The moon and the sun have both melted away.

I am no one. I am echo. I am gone.



And no one has noticed. Of course, no one has.



At last, the black forest begins to relent.

A shadow trips forward; I fall to the floor

Of a world wet with dew, and my spirit is bent

But broken wide open. My eyes see the door.



I enter. They stop. Every face is a stone.

Their expressions of disgust are a cold, bitter rain.

They see the old filth that the wild has not known,

And they back me away from my purpose again.



The door slams. The sound is the end of the world—

The crack of the planet when Thor’s hammer falls.



I turn from that echo. My feet are now hurled

Toward a new path that beckons and calls.

A crashing grows louder—a caged, angry water—

A river that rages and claws at its banks.

My focus finds light in the midst of the slaughter,

The one spot that shines in the midst of the ranks

Of the crushing, dark waves. I take the last step,

Not to cross, but to fall. To embrace and to yield.

My arms stretch to hug the whole world in its depth,

And the strands of my hair kiss my face on the field.



I am broken to pieces: to Earth and to Fire,

To Air and the Water that wanted me free.

My head rises up from the crash of the sirens,

And I breathe in the world. And the world breathes in me.
