Eye See You, I Spy

"Eye see you!" roared the Old Man’s voice,
His squint was sharp, he had no choice.
"I spy!" the mouse squeaked, bold and spry,
His tiny eyes lit up the sky.

The Old Man glared through dusty glass,
Saw crumbs and coins in blades of grass.
"I spot your tricks, your cheesy schemes!"
He bragged, his face all grins and gleams.

The mouse, he danced on tabletop,
"I spy your socks, your mop, your slop!
My peepers catch each nick and dime!"
He puffed his chest, his pride sublime.

They boasted loud, their eyes like hawks,
Saw rash and gash, old clocks and socks.
"I’m king of sight!" each claimed with glee,
Till both got lost in what they’d see.
