When Silence Becomes a Prison

They say that silence is golden bright,

But gold becomes a noose pulled tight—

A gilded chain around the throat

Of every undelivered note.



Silence is the assassin's blade

That cuts down truth before it's made,

The cancer growing in the bone

Of hearts that die while still alone.



It is the flood that drowns the scream,

The guillotine that ends the dream,

The poison poured in freedom's well,

The executioner's final bell.



In boardrooms where the vultures feast,

Silence feeds the hungry beast.

In homes where souls should dance and sing,

It clips the angel's broken wing.



The graveyard quiet after war,

When pride has locked and barred the door,

And mercy bleeds out on the floor

While silence stands guard evermore.



Children caged with muzzled mouths

Grow up as ghosts in haunted houses,

Their spirits crushed beneath the stone

Of voices turned to dust and bone.



The telephone becomes a tomb,

The door, a seal upon the room,

The questions buried six feet deep,

The truths that make the darkness weep.



Sometimes silence is the knife

That slowly bleeds away our life—

The vampire drinking at our veins,

The rust that eats away our chains.



So shatter silence like a curse,

Let chaos heal what quiet makes worse.

Better the storm than funeral calm,

Better the wound than numbing balm.
