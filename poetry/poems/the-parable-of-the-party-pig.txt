The Parable of the Party Pig

The barn was decked in pink and green,

A pig’s sweet sixteen to cheer,

Miss Porky twirled, a sight unseen,

Her Pork-ceanera was finally  here.



The cows brought gifts of hay and grass,

The chickens clucked in tune,

She snorted loud, a sassy lass,

Beneath a big balloon.



Her tiara gleamed on bristly hair,

The goats all clapped their hooves,

She ate her cake without a care,

Ignoring barnyard rules.



The rooster tried to cut the rug,

His dance was quite a flop,

Miss Porky laughed and gave a shrug,

Her party wouldn’t stop.



But then the mud called out her name,

She ditched the gown and crown,

Dove in with glee, no hint of shame,

The guests all wore a frown.



The moral here is plain to see,

Through glitter, glitz, and cheer,

Be true to you, just let it be,

Yourself’s what makes you dear.
