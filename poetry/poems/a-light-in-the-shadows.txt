A Light in the Shadows

In every heart, there's a wound that's hard to hide,

A scar that forms when love and trust divide.

We've all been hurt, we've all been worn down too,

But in our darkness, a spark of hope shines through.



We've felt the weight of silence, the sting of pain,

The ache of loneliness, the bitter taste of shame.

We've known the fear of unknowns, the dread of what's to come,

But in our hearts, a resilience whispers "you're not alone."



We've all been there, where shadows loom and fears collide,

Where doubts creep in like thieves, and fears take their ride.

But still we search for light, for solace and reprieve,

For a gentle breeze that soothes the heart, and helps us breathe.



In every soul, there's a spark that refuses to fade,

A flame of hope that flickers bright, even in the darkest shade.

It's the whisper of kindness, the warmth of love so true

That says "you are seen", "you are heard", and "you are loved too"



So let's hold on to this spark, this light that shines bright,

Let it guide us through the darkness, into a brighter night.

And though we'll stumble, though we'll fall to the ground

May love's gentle whisper rise up all around.
