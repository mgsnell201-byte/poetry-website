The Island Beckons

I anchored where the sea hums,

a lullaby of turquoise and time—

where the waves kneel

to kiss the shore,

their foamy lips whispering secrets

to the sand.



The sky, a bowl of endless blue,

holds no memory of clouds,

only the sun’s golden fingers

brushing my skin,

leaving warmth like a lover’s touch.



The trade winds come,

carrying the breath of jasmine—

a ghost of blossoms

dancing just beyond sight,

teasing the air

with promises of hidden groves.



The beach, untouched,

welcomes me like a returning tide.

The sand, fine as powdered silk,

yields beneath my feet,

each step a silent conversation

between wanderer and earth.



Here, the world is still,

save for the rustle of palm fronds

clapping softly in the breeze—

a slow, sacred applause

for the solitude,

for the sea,

for the moment

that belongs only to me.
