The Wood Shaper's Song

Beneath the rafters, old and brown,
Where shadows dance on timbered wall,
The craftsman toils, with furrowed brow,
His lathe hums softly, a steady call.

His beard, like snow on mountain peaks,
Falls over his apron, coarse and worn;
With knowing hands, that craft unique,
He shapes the wood, by labor sworn.

His hands caress each whirling grain,
The wood obeys his firm design;
Each cut, a whisper, soft and plain,
In every curve, his gifts align.

The tools around, in ordered line,
Bear witness to his steadfast art—
The knives, the chisels, sharp and fine,
Each piece a treasure in his heart.

His craft endures, a timeless flame,
Through years that test his steady hand;
With every turn, he stakes his claim,
A master of this wooden land.

Yet while he bends, with heart so true,
The scent of cedar fills the space,
The lathe's low song will see him through,
In every curve, his heart's embrace.
