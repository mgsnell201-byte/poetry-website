The Arsonist's Hymn

I struck the match—the World said "No—
The Flame must serve the Steeple—"
But Fire whispered "Dare to glow,
Burn down their golden People—"

The Gallows watched me raise my spark,
The Crowd cried "Heresy!"
Yet when I lit the waiting Dark—
The Stars leaned down to see—

Oblivion's Feast! I fed the Air
With all their sacred Letters—
While Ashes danced like answered Prayer
On Chains turned to Fetters—

They called it Crime—I called it Song—
That leapt from my small Finger—
Till every Thou Shalt Not was gone—
And I—the lingered Singer—

Now let them build their Temples high
With Rules of frost and Sorrow—
My Sun still hangs against their Sky—
And burns their Tomorrow—
