A  Lifeline

Hope is a flame that flickers in the dark
A spark that refuses to be extinguished

It's the whispered promise of a better tomorrow

The gentle breeze that rustles the leaves of our hearts

But hope can also be a weight that we bear
A burden that threatens to consume us whole

It's the longing gaze that fixes on a distant shore

Yet still, we hold on to hope
Like a lifeline tossed out by a stranger in need

We cling to its fragile thread
And pray that it will be enough

Hope is not always easy to find
But sometimes, in the darkest of nights
It's the soft whisper of a friend's voice

The gentle touch that soothes our souls
