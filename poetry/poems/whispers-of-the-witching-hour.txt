Whispers of the Witching Hour

At the midnight hour when shadows dance,

And jack-o'-lanterns cast their glance,

I found you standing 'neath the harvest moon,

Your costume sparkling like stars in June.



The witching hour had cast its spell,

As Autumn leaves around us fell.

Your fangs though false, with glimmer shone,

While furry ears marked me as mine.



Through cobwebbed paths and pumpkins glow,

Past children dressed as witches low,

We walked together, hand in hand,

Two souls who seemed to understand.



The church bells tolled their solemn chime,

As we defied both space and time.

Your laughter echoed sweet and clear,

Through veils between worlds, drawing near.



When morning threatened in the east,

And spirits fled their midnight feast,

You kept the mask but gave your heart,

A Halloween love never to depart.



Now every year when witches fly,

And black cats prowl beneath the sky,

I meet you at the midnight hour,

Beneath the old clock's haunted tower.



For love ignited under guise,

Like autumn stars in evening skies.

When midnight skies on Halloween,

Two hearts unveiled what love does mean.
