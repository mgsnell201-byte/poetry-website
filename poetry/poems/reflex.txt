Reflex

The knee kicks out, the pupils shrink—

the body acts before you think.

No pause for doubt, no room for grace,

just primal laws etched in your face.



The heart, though, has its own recoil:

a name once loved now burns like oil.

You swore you’d changed, yet here you stand,

still flinching at his ringless hand.



The crowd obeys an unseen shove,

each "yes" a trained refrain of love.

We call it choice, but pause, reflect:

how much of life is just reflex?
