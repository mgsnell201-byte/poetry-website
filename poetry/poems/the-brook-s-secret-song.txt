The Brook's Secret Song

A brook that chatters to the stones

In water-whispers, sings alone,

And tells of where the river goes

Beneath the arch where no one knows.



It speaks of fish with silver scales

And of the wind that tells it tales,

Of mossy banks and dragonflies

That watch with great and golden eyes.



So if you hear a liquid word

From that fast-flowing, small stream heard,

You'll know the secret it has sung

Since all the world was fresh and young.
