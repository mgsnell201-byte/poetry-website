The Emu's Bad Day

Out in the garden where the wattle burns gold,

An emu came strutting, cheeky and bold.

He sniffed at the boronia, sweet on the breeze,

Then flattened the mulla-mulla down to his knees.



"Bloody oath!" hollered Shirl, "would ya look at that bird,

He's mad as a cut snake" — and gee, she'd not erred.

He spun through the flowerbeds, wild and unchecked,

Not one sprig of wattle escaped with respect.



"That bird's got a roo loose in his top paddock, love,

Chasing his tail like there's no God above."
