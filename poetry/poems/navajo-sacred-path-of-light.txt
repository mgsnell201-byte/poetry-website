Navajo Sacred Path of Light

In the east, where dawn first breaks,
Yoolgai  shines pure and white,
While sacred colors start to wake
On pathways of the light.

To southern skies where turquoise gleams,
Dootl'izh's sacred blue,
The rainbow's ancient flowing beams
Shine forth forever true.

In western gold, the yellow sun
Makes diichili glow,
As sacred colors softly run
Between above, below.

The northern darkness brings its peace
With bááshzhinii's might,
Where rainbow colors find release
Through starlit roads at night.

Upon this bridge of sacred sky
Between the worlds we knew,
The colors dance and multiply
In paths of sacred hue.

The Wind bears prayers as we gaze
Upon their rainbow trail,
Where Holy Ones in ancient days
Drew back the sacred veil.

Through four directions, bright and clear,
Their rainbow path descends,
Reminding us they're ever near
As heaven earthward bends.
