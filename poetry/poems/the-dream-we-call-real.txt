The Dream We Call Real

We wake each morning into dream,
Mistaking shadows for the beam
Of truth that never cracks the night—
Are certainties just tricks of light?

The ground we swear is firm and sweet
Shifts like dunes in windblown heat.
What we clutch dissolves like mist;
What we claim does not exist.

We build our homes and plant our trees,
Build our truths on trembling pleas,
While time dissolves each careful plan—
The dreamer dreams, but where's the man?

Behind the veil of what seems real
Are players we can barely feel,
Yet still convinced of what we show:
The heart's the only truth we know.

But sometimes, in the quiet space
Between two breaths, we find our place,
The dreamer stirs—truth shows its face,
Not waking, but remembering grace:

The hand that dreams, the dream it spins,
The place where every ending begins,
The sand, the star, the breath we take—
All one, before we fully wake.

Then dream, but with a looser hand,
Let tides erase your script in sand,
And love the ghost you can't undo—
The star that burns you through and through.
