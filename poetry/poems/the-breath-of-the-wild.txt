The Breath of the Wild

The wind whispers secrets through sun-dappled boughs,
A sigh, a swish, a sizzle-shuffle now.
Leaves, emerald whispers, pirouette and sway,
A ballet born of breezes all the day.

Babbling brooks, with pebbles click and clack,
A gurgling chorus, nature's rhythmic act.
Water whispers, tinkles, tumbles, sings,
A crystal symphony from hidden springs.

Then, thunderous hush, a curtain drawn aside,
A waterfall explodes in diamond tide.
Crashing, roaring, waters leap and churn,
A foamy frenzy, wild and newly born.

But wait, a hush, a whisper soft and low,
The torrent sighs, a lullaby aglow.
Wind lulls the leaves, the stream serenades,
And sunlight paints the forest in arcades.

Nature's orchestra, a symphony complete,
From rustling leaf to waterfall's retreat.
A chorus sung in whispers, sighs, and booms,
A lullaby of earth in verdant rooms.
