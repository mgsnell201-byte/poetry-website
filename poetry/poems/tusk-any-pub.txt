Tusk-any Pub

An elephant, walrus, and manatee strolled in,

Two with mighty tusks, one with nubs smooth, all set to begin.

They ordered pot pies, steaming, golden, round,

The best Tusk-any fare that could be found.



The elephant reared up, tusks curved like scimitars grand,

"These prove I'm built for the Tuscan land!"

The walrus nodded, tusks gleaming, thick and bold,

"Mine match yours, mate—we're the Tusk-any gold!"



The manatee just settled deeper in the chair,

No tusks to flash, no boasts to share.

"I've got nubs, instead, and that's just fine—

I'm here for the crust, the gravy, the wine."



While the tusked two dreamed of vineyards and sun,

The smooth-faced one devoured every crumb.

Tusks may impress with their curve and their gleam,

But nubs let you eat without making a scene.



So here's the real moral, fresh from the pub:

Tusks bring the swagger, but nubs bring the grub!

The tusked ones talk Tuscany till voices are hoarse—

The manatee just smiles and orders seconds, of course!
