Sanctuary of Twilight

The sky’s amber bleeds through the wound of the day,

As the ocean reflects all the light that’s to fade,

Each wave whispers soft as a prayer’s gentle sway,

To the hush of the night in its sacred cascade.



In this cradle of sand where the sea softly flows,

I’m the stillness the world in its rush left behind,

A seed sown in silence where vastness bestows,

Where time’s tangles unravel and peace I can find.



The breeze brushes through me like love’s tender breath,

Salt-kissed as a mother’s sweet song in the air,

While gulls trace their hymns through the sky’s boundless depth,

Holding me in its strength with a beauty so fair



These colors might be how the divine chooses speech—

Rose gold with the copper and purple entwined,

A canvas that stretches from peak unto peak,

A vision of splendor for my eyes alone signed.



The waves stroke the shore as they lull it to rest,

Their rhythm my heartbeat now slowing with ease,

As earth breathes in quiet, its pulse manifest,

I’m both silence and sound in this moment’s release.



This instant’s a church with no walls to confine,

Each grain of the sand holds a prayer I have spun,

Where peace falls like evening in soft, gentle line,

And fear finds its rest when the day is undone.



I witness this hour when the day yields to night,

In surrender I find there’s a power to claim,

To be both the dark and the glow of the light,

In twilight’s embrace where I’m whole once again.
