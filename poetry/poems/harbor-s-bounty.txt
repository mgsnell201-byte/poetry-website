Harbor's Bounty

Down by the weathered dock where seagulls call,
In a tavern where the salty breezes fall,
Creamy waves of white fill up my bowl,
Like morning fog that soothes a sailor's soul.

Tender potatoes drift and gently roll,
Like ocean pearls within this warming whole,
While celery dances crisp and ocean-green,
The finest marsh-born stalks I've ever seen.

Onions shimmer clear as polished glass,
Sweet as the tides that through the harbor pass,
And there beneath the surface, clams reside,
Briny bivalves gathered from the morning tide.

Smoky bacon tells its salty tale,
Of farms that meet the ocean's windy gale,
The bowl grows warm like a lighthouse in my hand,
While harbor bells ring out across the land.

Each spoonful holds the ocean's ancient song,
Rich cream embraces all, both sweet and strong,
Here where the fishermen have shared their catch,
Since wooden ships first found this perfect match.

The chowder house echoes with briny lore,
This bowl of treasures from the ancient shore—
Where sea and land dance on my thankful tongue,
Clam chowder is my favorite one.
