The Boot-i-ful Lady

She's planted in a boot-i-sole,
No longer on a walking stroll.
Her skin is leath-ery and creased,
Her wandering has been de-leased.

The laces wind like vein-y thread,
A garden for her ankle-bed.
She’s partly boot and part-ly she,
A boot-an-ist growing happily.

Does she miss the step-by-step?
Or love her root-ed rest and pep?
She's grown into her leather home—
No need to walk, no need to roam.

She's part-ly boot and part-ly she,
A plant-ed human happily.
The sole-mate that she'd always sought—
A place to stay, forever caught!
