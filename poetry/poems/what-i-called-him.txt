What I Called Him

Me and Tommy had a fight
about whose turn it was to write
the answer on the chalkboard first,
so I let him have my very worst.

"You're an ALAPAZEL, through and through!"
(I made that word up, but it's true.)
His face went red, he stomped his shoe,
and hollered back, "Well, YOU'RE one too!"

"BOONEYDONDLE!" I said next,
the ugliest word I know, in text,
he gasped so hard he dropped his chalk
and couldn't even find his walk.

"CHUCKLECHAZEL butt" I roared,
the meanest thing I could afford,
he called me something worse, I'm sure,
but I forgot it — that's for sure.

"DOODERBEEDERBOB!" I hollered last,
the biggest word from all my past,
he blinked, he paused, he had to grin—
"That one's too good. You win."
