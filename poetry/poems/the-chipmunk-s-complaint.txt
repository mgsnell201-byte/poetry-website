The Chipmunk's Complaint

Now WHY should I go out tonight

When everything in here's just right?

My green chair hugs me like a mitt,

This plaid blanket's a perfect fit!



I've got my pile of sunflower seeds

(That's ALL a cozy chipmunk needs),

The kettle's singing its red song,

The stove's been crackling all day long.



There's smoke and tea-smell in the air,

A mushroom sprouting by my chair,

Some autumn leaves I dragged inside,

A spider friend who likes to hide.



But YOU say, "Chip, go climb a tree!

Go scamper! Leap! Run wild and free!"

Well, I say nuts to all of that—

I'm staying here. And that is THAT.
