All Aboard the GHOUL BUS

Green-skinned rider in a witch’s hat waves hello,
Hairy wolf and caped vampire ride along for the show.
Orange wheels on the bus go round and round,
Under a ghost on the roof, who haunts without a sound.
Leaves swirl past the porch like ghosts from a tomb,

Boarding is open, there’s plenty of room!
Up the steps and climb aboard,
Shrieking with laughter, the whole haunted horde!
