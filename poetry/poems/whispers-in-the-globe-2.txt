Whispers in the Globe

In a globe of glass, suspended high,

A man of fear, with muffled sigh,

Built his world, a fragile shell,

From worries spun, a fearsome spell.



No dreams pursued, no oceans charted,

No love's embrace, no victory harbored.

He spun a web, of fragile light,

To shield his heart, from doubts that bite.



Distant lives, a song unheard,

Where laughter lights, he finds no word.

Their smiles ignite, they dance, they roam,

While whispered phantoms claim his home.



But terror's grip, a chilling vice,

Holds him captive, pays no price.

He huddles close, a trembling form,

Afraid to weather life's warm storm.



Frost-kissed sphere, where dreams grow cold,

Will hope ignite, a story told?

No sunlit touch, no laughter's chime,

Can he escape, and claim his time?



The wind picks up, a whisper near,

He feels the walls, the shifting fear.

Will he stay bound, or take the flight?

The fragile orb hangs in the light.
