I Beg Your Pardon

I beg your pardon, Mr. President,

Did I hear my name today?

Yes, they call me Drumstick, sir,

And I’m saved, I’m glad to say!



I beg your pardon, brother birds,

For being the lucky one,

While you’re headed for the oven

To be roasted, stuffed and done.



So thank you for the pardon, sir,

And pardon me, I pray,

If I’m not on your table

On this Thanksgiving Day!
