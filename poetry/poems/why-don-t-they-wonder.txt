Why Don't They Wonder

Grown-ups say the sky is blue,
but do they see it like I do?
Do they stop and stare and wonder why
the clouds are sheep that float so high?

Why do they walk so fast, so straight,
and never stop to investigate
the ants that march in tiny lines,
or how the sun on water shines?

Why do they say, "Don’t ask too much,"
when every rock and leaf and such
holds secrets big and wild and true—
like magic hiding in plain view?

And why, when someone’s not the same,
do grown-ups whisper, call them names?
Don’t they know that hearts can break
like glass that falls but can’t remake?

The world’s so wide! So bright! So new!
Why don’t they love it like I do?
Maybe they just… forgot to play,
and lost the wonder on the way.
