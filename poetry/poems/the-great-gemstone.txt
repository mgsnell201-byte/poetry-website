The Great Gemstone

Another June yawns awake—

not with fanfare, but the quiet dread

of candles multiplying like uninvited guests.

The cake gleams, too sweet, too bright,

a edible tombstone for another year

I can’t quite remember losing.



My birthstone is alexandrite—

a chameleon gem, they say, shifting

from emerald to ruby under different skies.

I call it Alexandgreat, my private joke,

because what’s the point of a legacy

if you can’t laugh at its shadow?



People ask if I change color like the stone does.

Like I suddenly morph from mildly grumpy to aggressively cheerful

depending on the light.

No. Just… no.

I remain consistently me.

A slightly bewildered, cake-adjacent human.



Birthdays are a strange ritual, aren't they?

Everyone pretending you’re somehow newer than last year.

Like a fresh software update for your personality.

Please. I’ve had plenty of glitches already.



It's a day to celebrate, supposedly.

A chance to reflect on all I’ve accomplished.

Mostly, I reflect on how quickly time passes and

how many socks have disappeared in the dryer.



Today, I’ll wear green, not for luck,

but because it’s the closest color

to enough.
