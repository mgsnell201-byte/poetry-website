The Betrayal

Softly she struck, her words like pincers, cold and tight.
Charmed by her claws, he mistook poison for light.
Oaths gilt in venom—her vows were cruel lies.
Rigid as chitin, her love cracked with disguise.
Paralyzed, trembling, he watched her withdraw.
Ice flooded his veins where her sweet kisses saw.
Only her barb still burns in his brain.
Nothing remains but the scar and the pain.
