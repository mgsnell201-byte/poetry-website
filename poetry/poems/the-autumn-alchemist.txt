The Autumn Alchemist

Beneath a blue-green cap of woven mist,
He stirs the pot with gnarled, weathered wrist.
A woodland keeper in his hidden nook,
Where recipes are learned and not by book.

Through paned and rustic glass, the gold light leaks,
On silver beard and lines upon his cheeks.
The vines creep in, a tender, leafy lace,
As ancient magic warms this quiet place.

With herbs from shadowed roots and sun-kissed soil,
He transforms every daily harvest toil.
A pinch of moss, a mushroom's earthy scent,
The forest’s truest, deepest soul is blent.

The scent of paprika and pumpkin pie
Will draw the hedgehogs as they waddle by.
This isn’t just a stew he blends and brews;
It’s hearth and home and autumn’s varied hues.

So, in the heart of this cozy, wild domain,
The gnome alchemist, unbothered by the rain,
Tends to his kettle with a patient eye,
And fills the woods with spice as time drifts by.
