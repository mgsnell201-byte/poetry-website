Palimpsest

They wrote in ink, but not to last—
Each word a veil, each phrase a mask.
Beneath the script, the ghost remains,
A deeper text in phantom stains.

The rose in verse, a thorn concealed—
Its petals speak, but wounds revealed.
The raven's cry in midnight dread,
Unspoken lore in feathers spread.

The parchment bears a thousand hands,
Each scraping truth to meet demands.
A symbol veiled, a truth confined—
Yet underneath, the soul's design.

We learn to read the buried scroll,
The tremor tucked inside "control,"
The way a pause can split a vow,
Or silence mark the sacred now.

The sonnet’s turn, a breath withheld,
The haiku’s hush where grief is spelled—
All layered in a quiet war,
Of meaning lost and metaphor.

Words fade like breath in winter's frost,
Their echoes fleeting, quickly lost.
But feelings carve a lasting groove,
Their silent weight no time can move.

For language is a borrowed skin,
Where deeper stories press within.
And those who learn to look askew
May find their own reflection too.
