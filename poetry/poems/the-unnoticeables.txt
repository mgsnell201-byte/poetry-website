The Unnoticeables

The robin sings

Each morning

Right outside your window—

But you're scrolling,

Scrolling,

Scrolling through your phone.



The clouds make shapes:

A dragon!

A sailing ship!

A giant's face!

But you're looking down,

Down,

Down at the sidewalk cracks.



Your dog wags his tail

Wag-wag-wag-wag-wag

Every time you enter—

Pure joy on four paws!

But you're thinking about

Dinner,

Laundry,

Bills,

Tomorrow's meeting.



The stars come out

Twinkle-ific!

Spark-tacular!

A whole universe

Winking hello—

But you've already

Gone inside,

Curtains closed,

Lights on.



And love?

Love taps you on the shoulder,

Whispers in your ear,

Leaves notes in your lunchbox,

Remembers how you like your coffee—

But you're too busy

Being busy

To notice

You're noticed,

To hear

You're heard,

To see

You're seen,

To know

You're loved-loved-LOVED

Every single day.



Maybe tomorrow

You'll look up,

Listen up,

Wake up

To all the wonder

Wondering

Why you never

Wonder back.
