The Fountain

I am a FOUN-
            TAIN
                 spilling
                        down
                             and
                                round!

My water feels like liquid laughter,
Cool as jokes told ever after,
Ticklish-tricky,
Splishy-splashy,
Gurgly-giggly,
Never crashy.

What I Hold:

One thousand ripples
Fifty wiggles
Seventeen fish-wishes
Countless liquid squiggles

The robins come for robin-baths,
They scrub their wings and do the splash!
The sparrows sip with tiny beaks—
My basin's where the thirsty seeks.

plip
plop
plink
I'm everybody's
favorite
drink!

The butterflies go flutter-by
And catch my mist up in the sky,
Their wings go whisper-whisper-wet
The quietest applause I get.

How do I hold it all inside?
With STONE so strong!
With BASIN wide!
I cup my palms like this: ( )
And never ever
let
it
miss.

At night the bats come SWOOPY-LOW,
They lick my surface—gulp and go!
No time for baths,
No time to think,
Just SLURP and vanish
in a blink.

I am magnifi-fountain!
I am aqua-mountain!
I am drip-drop never-stopping
Every splashy wish worth granting—

From dawn to dark
From lark to bat
I hold my cool
And that
is
that.
