Surely you Jest

At a table set brightly with mismatched delight,

The Mad Hatter poured tea, though it felt rather slight.

“Would you care for some treacle?” he asked with a grin,

While the Dormouse, quite sleepy, was dozing within.



“Draw treacle from water?” young Alice exclaimed,

“Are you mad or just silly? The thought should be famed!”

The Hatter chimed in, with a chuckle and fuss,

“Well, you draw from a well—not from well, it’s a plus!”



“Oh, you surely draw from the depths of your mind,

To conjure such nonsense, I’m wholly aligned!”

The Dormouse awoke with a yawn and a stretch,

“Muchness is all, in this world — that I sketch."



“Sketch? What do you mean?” Alice puzzled, bemused,

“Much of a muchness?” she added, confused.

“Indeed, we draw memories; we draw them with glee,

Like drawing a mouse with a trap on your knee!”



But before she could ponder the meaning behind,

A new figure approached, with curiosity twined.

He was named Quirkle, with eyes full of jest,

“And I come with a riddle, for you all to test!”



With a grin like the Cheshire, and riddles a-plenty,

He spoke of contradictions, delightful yet risky.

“A hair of the dog that once wagged, now sits,

For what’s lost in the forest is found in the pits.”



The Hatter looked askance and the Dormouse did snore,

“Do you think we have time for this riddle in store?”

“Oh, but dear friends!” cried young Quirkle with glee,

“To find sense in nonsense is key, can’t you see?”



“Will I find what I seek in your riddles, dear friend?

Or will this chaotic tea party never quite end?”

With each twist of the words, Alice’s head spun,

For though she wanted clarity, she hardly had fun.



Success, she thought, must be buried beneath,

This eccentric exchange, like a riddle with teeth.

As the Dormouse was dunked in a whirlpool of tea,

Quirkle just chuckled, “It’s quite a sight, wouldn’t you agree?”



With a nudge of the teapot and a flick of his wrist,

He whispered, “The answers are there — you just missed.”

The tea party’s madness, so wild and profound,

Held lessons to savor, if one looked all around.



So Alice resolved, amid muchness of chat,

To seek hidden meanings in riddles like that.

With Quirkle beside her, a guide of sorts fine,

Her journey through Wonderland continued to twine.



Where puns danced like leaves and logic took flight,

In the land of the curious, day merged into night.

And amid all the chaos, with courage she’d tread,

To unlock all the wonders that lay just ahead.
