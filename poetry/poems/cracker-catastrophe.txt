Cracker Catastrophe

Mr. Macaw: (Puffing out his chest)

"This cracker's mine, a beakful delight!

Green feathers and envy, take flight!"



Mr. Green: (Eyes narrowed)

"Not so fast, you squawking buffoon!

My turn to munch, beneath the moon!"



They squawk and they squabble, a feathery flurry,

But the cracker crumbles, in feathered hurry.



Both sigh, beaks empty, a comical sight,

"Next time, we'll share," they mutter, taking flight.
