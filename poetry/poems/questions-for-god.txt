Questions for God

In cities of gloom where the lost wander blind,

I ask God, “Why’s truth so darn hard to find?”

Their steps echo grief through a fog thick with dread,

No map lights their path where despair’s coldly led.



A father holds tight to his son’s fevered hand,

He prays, “Lord, why’s healing beyond my command?”

The night steals his breath, and the dawn brings no cure,

How’s faith meant to stand when such pain must endure?



Two lovers once swore they’d forever be true,

I cry, “God, why’s love just a ghost passing through?”

Their promises fade into silence and ache,

What’s left when their hearts find no mend for the break?



On streets cracked by hate, where the angry divide,

I plead, “Lord, why’s peace pushed so far to the side?”

Words clash like sharp swords, tearing hope’s fragile seam,

Who’ll stitch back the world to its long-cherished dream?



A girl sits alone with a weight none can see,

I ask, “God, what shadow makes joy bend its knee?”

Her smile hides a storm that no comfort can still,

How’s light meant to spark on her soul’s darkened hill?



Through this storm of questions, my cries pierce the sky,

I shout, “God, why’s torment the weight we can’t pry?”

If pain shapes our fate, is Your answer too far?

A voice shakes the dark: “I’m the hope in your scar.”
