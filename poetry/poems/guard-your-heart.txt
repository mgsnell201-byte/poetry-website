Guard Your Heart

Based on Proverbs 4:23: "Above all the things that you guard, safeguard your heart, for out of it are the sources of life."

In chambers deep where thoughts take flight,
Where dreams are born and fears take root,
Lives a garden, precious, bright—
The heart that bears life's sweetest fruit.

Guard it well from bitter seeds
That poisonous tongues might try to sow,
From envy's grasp and hatred's deeds
That make the springs of goodness slow.

For what we feed within this space
Will color every word we speak,
Will shape the love upon our face
And guide the kindness that we seek.

The heart's a wellspring, pure and true,
When tended with a careful hand,
But let corruption seep on through
And poison spreads throughout the land.

So build high walls of wisdom strong,
Let truth stand guard at every gate,
Choose carefully what you let along—
Your heart determines your whole fate.

Above all treasures, silver, gold,
Above all riches you might win,
Guard well the stories your heart holds,
For life itself flows from within.
