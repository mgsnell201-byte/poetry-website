Viper's Lullaby

A new year dawns, viper's hiss and barbwire lulled to sleep,
Ink of war fades, silken dreams the open canvas steep.
No iron hearts, but palms like sun, where healing blossoms bloom,
No fear-etched lines, just stardust paths, chasing shadows from the room.

War drums slumber, hushed by trills on emerald wings,
Where echoes chilled, now wildflowers rise, bold love their message sings.
Beneath a sun that binds the torn, a butterfly takes flight,
A fragile peace on silken wings, bathed in forgiving light.
