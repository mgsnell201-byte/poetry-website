Dreamland's Gentle Embrace - A Lullaby

Softly now, my precious one,

Day is done, the night's begun.

Moonbeams kiss your sleepy eyes,

While the stars sing lullabies.



Breathe in peace, so warm and light,

Breathe out all the day's last light.

Golden dreams will find you soon,

Like a sweet and tender tune.



Rest now, darling, drift away,

On a cloud of cotton gray.

Angels guard you, love surrounds,

Safe in whispers, softest sounds...



Pillows cradle, blankets fold,

Wrapping you in dreams of gold.

Gentle breezes hum so low,

"Little one, it's time to go..."



Slower now, and slower still,

Heartbeats calm, the world grows still.

Nothing here but love and light,

Holding you through all the night.



Rest now, darling, drift away,

On a cloud of cotton gray.

Angels guard you, love surrounds,

Safe in whispers, softest sounds...



Go to sleep.

And dream so deep
