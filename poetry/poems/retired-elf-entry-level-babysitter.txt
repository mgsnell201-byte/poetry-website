Retired Elf, Entry-Level Babysitter

I planned my elven twilight years,
With dragonets—no fuss, no fears.
A peaceful post. A gentle breeze.
Instead: burned beard and singed-off knees.

They climb me like a climbing wall,
Use tail as rope (I always fall).
My hat's aflame, my shoes are toast—
I'm not a GUARD—I'm just their POST.

The red spots on each toadstool cap?
Not nature's paint—a fire-y zap!
When dragonets begin to sneeze,
They torch the mushrooms, grass, and trees.

"Dignified," the job ad said.
Now I'm a jungle gym instead.
With scales that cling and claws that grip—
Retirement? HA! What a trip.

No personal space, no quiet time,
Just dragonets who love to climb.
I dreamed of calm, of rest—then whoosh!
My eyebrows? Gone. My pride? All mush.
