Time's Gentle Whisper

I walk through time's long winding path today,
While wisdom lights each step along my way;
Youth's memories fade like stars at break of morn,
As time leaves marks that age has gently worn.

Through veins, time's currents flow with memories old,
While laughter from the past its tales unfold;
Like autumn leaves, my friends have drifted way,
Yet bonds we forged still bloom with each new day.

Grey hairs upon my crown show paths I've trod,
Each line my face now bears speaks words from God;
Though time's swift hands I cannot turn or stay,
My soul finds peace in life's sweet ebb and sway.

The years march on, and still I grow with grace,
Each day brings wisdom at its gentle pace;
This precious life's a gift I treasure dear,
Like sunsets painting hope through out the year.
