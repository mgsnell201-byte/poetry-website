The World is a Playground

They say childhood ends when you finally grow.
They say you stop dreaming and just… let it go.
They say you stop running through puddles and such.
I'm not going to. Thanks very much.

There's something beneath my bed, something with claws,
something with twelve fuzzy feet and six jaws.
My dad says there's nothing — he's probably right.
I'm sleeping at his place, just for tonight.

They say I must go to bed at eight,
but my eyes won't sleep — they want to stay up late.
My feet won't sleep, my nose won't either,
and my brain's wide awake — it's a total non-sleeper.
So I sent all my parts a very firm note
that said "GO TO SLEEP" — but my hand wrote the quote.
