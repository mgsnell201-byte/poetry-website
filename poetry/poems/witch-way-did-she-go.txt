Witch Way did she Go?

"Which way did she go?" asked the troll with the squint,
His eyebrow as bushy as bark on a flint.
"She zoomed past like thunder on tires of oak!"
And left a big cloud of frog-scented smoke.

"The WITCH way!" his partner cried, pointing his claw,
At chrome-plated cauldron the fastest they saw.
Her broomstick had handlebars, fuzzy and green,
The wildest of witches they'd ever had seen!

She hollered at treetops, she honked at the moon,
Her potion jars rattling a clattering tune.
The frogs in her saddlebag croaked out a song,
While mushrooms and moonbeams went bouncing along.

The trolls stood there gaping, their mouths open wide,
As glitter and bat-wings flew out from each side.
"I waved UP," said the first troll, "but she flew right past."
"Wave DOWN," said the second, "or we'll be her repast!"
