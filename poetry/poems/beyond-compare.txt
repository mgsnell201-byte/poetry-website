Beyond Compare

In life's vast garden, seeds are sown,
Each child a sprout, uniquely grown.
Nourished by love, with care and time,
Their roots take hold, their petals climb.

Yet not all bloom at equal pace,
Each has their own rhythmic grace.
Some burst forth with morning dew,
While others bide, their time to break through.

Resist the urge to make compare,
As if one bloom is beyond compare.
For nature's grace flows free and fair,
On every petal, without care.

A wise gardener knows what each needs,
To flourish from their buried seeds.
Provide the light, the soil, the rain -
Then watch them grow, with gentle gain.

For in this garden, vast and grand,
Each flower adds its own demand.
Together weaving nature's art,
A harmony of mind and heart.
