The alarm clock rings – that fiendish thing,
A modern torture, clear and true.
My pillows mock the pain it brings,
As comfort bids its last adieu.

What fool invented morning time?
What creature thrives at break of day?
To rise before the coffee's prime –
A punishment, I dare to say!

My feet, they drag across cold floors,
Like chains that Marley's ghost once bore,
While sleep-drunk stumbling through my doors,
Each step a fresh new form of war.

So spare me all your chirping birds,
Your "good" before my "morning," pray.
Until that brew has been procured,
Keep all your cheerful thoughts at bay!

For in this state of pre-caffeine,
Like Scrooge among his ghostly fears,
The world's a bleak and bitter scene,
And joy itself draws bitter tears!

But lo! The coffee pot doth steam...
(Though transformation's not quite yet)
Until that first sip I redeem,
I curse this morning with regret!
