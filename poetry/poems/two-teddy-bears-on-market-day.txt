Two Teddy Bears on Market Day

You know how sometimes you look out the window

and the ordinary street has decided to be ridiculous

just for a moment, as if life got tired of being itself

and put on a little costume?



That's what happened this evening in the village

when two teddy bears appeared on the cobblestones,

walking side by side like old neighbors returning from errands.

One carried two bulging bags of groceries—

you could hear the faint clink of jam jars knocking together,

a small percussion section announcing abundance—

the other held a bouquet of yellow tulips

bright as fresh paint against limestone walls.



Their fur was the same soft gray-brown

as the weathered cottages they passed,

so they seemed less like visitors

and more like the village itself had grown legs

and gone shopping. Their sweaters—

one forest green, the other oatmeal—

blended with the moss and the stone,

as if the landscape had dressed them

before letting them out the door.



From down here at paw level,

the cobblestones rise like a miniature Alps,

each rounded peak a small challenge

for their polished boots to navigate.

The wicker handles bite gently into plush paws,

the baguette crust rasps against velvet pads—

a little friction, a small conversation

between the baker's hard work and their grip.



And the silence—oh, the silence of fur

is something humans can only envy.

No footsteps to announce them,

only the soft scritch-scritch of wool sleeves

rubbing as they waddle along,

and the low, gravelly hum

the taller one makes, vibrating deep

like the village settling into dusk.



The tulips aren't just flowers;

they're tiny lanterns the second bear carries

to push back the gray shadows creeping in,

while the groceries speak of pantries and suppers,

of ordinary evenings that somehow matter.



I watch them go, these stitched ambassadors

of sturdiness and softness,

and I wonder if that's the secret we've been missing—

to dress warmly against the damp air,

to carry both the bread and the brightness,

to belong so completely to a place

that your fur matches its stones.



Maybe that's why they look so content,

so quietly amused at the whole arrangement.

They know the village provides the setting,

and they provide the heart that carries it home.
