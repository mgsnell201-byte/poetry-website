Pom-Era-Rain-ians

They say it’s raining cats and dogs,
But look what’s on the couch:
Seven fluffy raindrops fell
And landed in a slouch!

Two black clouds, so dark and sleek,
Roll in with stormy flair,
While three white flurries, soft as snow,
Drift down without a care.

A white-and-tan one, sunset-bright,
Peeks out between the showers,
And one mottled, misty pup
Adds mystery to the hours.

All seven stare right at the lens,
Each face a little storm,
But one white puff tilts up her head
And asks, “Is this the norm?”

Pom-era-rain-ians, every one,
A forecast I adore:
Seven puffs of pouring joy,
And I would take some more!
