The Dragon's Spotted Dance

A dragon with a coat of dots

Begins to dance on grassy spots.

He hears a tune the wind has played

Beneath the sun and moon's arcade.



His polka is a happy sound

That makes the trees sway all around.

The spotted wings begin to beat

In time with his four-clawed feet.



A badger peeks out from his den

And joins the dance, then goes again.

The forest holds its breath in awe

To witness this unwritten law.



That magic is not fierce or grim,

But found in such a joyful whim.
