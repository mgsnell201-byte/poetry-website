The Misfit Parade

A hum-hum-hums inside my chest,

A radio that never rests.

While briefcase people rush on by,

I'm tuned to a PECULIAR sky.



I walk through alleys, brick and gray,

Past spray-paint names that fade away.

I feel like I'm a stranger-guest,

Un-easy in my own skin-vest.



Then BEAR appears beside my feet,

With heavy paws and furnace heat!

A raven perched up-on a post,

They know me like a friendly ghost.



No questions asked, no forms to fill—

They sense my blue, they feel my chill.

The raccoon grabs my scattered thoughts,

The fox keeps step through parking lots.



I wasn't lost, just out of place,

Till creatures came and matched my pace.

This wild-tribe found me by my sound—

No map required to come around!
