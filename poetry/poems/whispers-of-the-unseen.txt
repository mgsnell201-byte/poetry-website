Whispers of the Unseen

Beneath the moon’s veiled gaze,

stars hum secrets no ear can catch,

their light threading through shadows

like threads of a forgotten dream.



The cats perch silent,

eyes glinting with riddles,

their tails curling into question marks

against the mushroom’s cryptic glow—

pillars of earth holding up the unknown.



Leaves fall, not in surrender,

but as messengers from a hidden realm,

carrying whispers of what lies beneath,

beneath the soil, beneath the skin of night.



The forest breathes in riddles,

its colors shifting like thoughts

too vast to name—

reds and golds swirling into darkness,

a tapestry woven by invisible hands.



Here, the unseen pulses,

a heartbeat in the stillness,

inviting me to step closer,

to unravel the mystery

that lives just beyond the reach of sight.
