Love Is

Love is the sun’s first light, spilling gold on silent hills,
a touch like petals drifting, soft upon your skin.
It’s the quiet hum of dawn before the world awakes,
a secret only hearts can hear, a melody within.

Love is the smile that lingers, like a flame in winter’s hold,
a spark that needs no kindling, yet sets the soul ablaze.
It’s the brush of fingertips—a language without sound,
a map of constellations traced in each other’s gaze.

Love is the rain that falls when every storm has passed,
not thunder, not a tempest, but whispers on the leaves.
It’s the way the earth remembers every seed it’s ever grown,
the way the ocean keeps the moon though daylight pulls and grieves.

Love is the quiet hand that holds you in the dark,
not chains, but roots that deepen where no eyes can see.
It’s the breath between two words that need not be spoken,
the gravity of planets, spinning wild yet free.

Love is the echo in the hollow of your ribs,
a hymn the blood remembers, though the mind forgets the tune.
It’s the way the world grows brighter when you step into the room—
not fire, not a lightning strike, but the steady glow of the moon.
