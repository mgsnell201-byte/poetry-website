Against the Drift - (Frickin Floppy Fish)

In the shallows, where the water runs low,

Something flops, a wild and messy pile.

Not smooth, not still, it smacks the air a blow,

Slapping hard on mud, fighting all the while.



It twists like it doesn’t know how to stop,

Bouncing loud where the wet turns to dry.

The stream pulls back, not caring what it’s got,

But this thing keeps flopping, refusing to lie.



Once, it ruled the deep, quick and bold,

Darting free where the currents play.

Now it flops in muck, weak and old,

Too tough to die, too worn to sway.



Its eyes, cloudy but locked, face the dry,

Not big, not done, just holding tight.

Each smack a grunt, each jump a try,

A soaked mess that won’t give up the fight.



The water moves on, not slowing down,

While this heap flaps, ragged and sore.

No big finish, no safe ground,

Just battling on, like it did before.
