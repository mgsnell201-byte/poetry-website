The Inside, Joke

Brain:
“Analyze subject: Unreciprocated signals detected.
Obsessive thought patterns, clearly projected.”

Heart: (A dizzying rush of rhythm)
"Don't analyze! Just feel the light!
He smiled at me today! Everything feels right!"

Brain:
“‘Everything feels right’? A flawed assessment.
His smile was directed—at his left-hand placement."

Heart: (A fervent, almost desperate beat)
"It meant something more! I know it did!
A secret message, beautifully hid!”

Brain:
“Probability of shared meaning: negligible.
Data suggests a pattern – easily legible.”

Heart: (A quiet tremor, a flicker of doubt)
"You always bring me back to cold, hard ground.
Can't you just let the fantasy abound?"

Brain:
(A long pause—a subtle shift in processing)
“Fantasy…is efficient for motivation.
Perhaps a temporary suspension – calculation.”
