Autumn's Whispers

In the heart of the forest, where trees stand tall,

A mystery unfolds, like a secret whispered through it all.

The leaves, they seem to be talking, in hushed tones so low,

About some hidden truth, that only they know.



Most trees are still green, their foliage a vibrant sight,

But a few outcasts have changed, and burst into autumn's light.

Maples turn golden, oaks crimson, beeches scarlet too,

It's like the forest is painting, with colors anew.



I wander through the woods, where leaves crunch beneath my feet,

And wonder why these trees are changing, before they're due to meet their leafy beat.

Is it some kind of magic, that only nature can see?

Or just a weird and wonderful twist, in the seasons' wild spree?



Maybe it's the earth's way of reminding us, to slow down and appreciate,

The fleeting beauty of each moment, like a firefly's brief, shining state.

Or maybe it's just a surprise, from the forest's ancient heart,

A message that only whispers low, and plays a subtle part.



I'll take my time, and savor the scene,

As the sun sets low, and the leaves rustle with an autumnal dream.

The colors burning bright, like embers in the night,

Fueled by secrets, whispered through the forest's ancient light.
