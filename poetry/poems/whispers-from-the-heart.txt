Whispers from the Heart

If you could hear me in your dreams, it wouldn't be a roar,

No trumpet blast, no crashing waves that pound upon the shore.

It wouldn't be a shout, no whispered plea, no siren's call,

But quiet music, like a brook that tumbles over thrall.



You'd hear the chime of laughter, soft and light, like wind in chimes,

A melody of sunshine, dancing through the passing times.

You'd hear the rustle of contentment, leaves adrift on summer breeze,

A sigh of peace, a whispered song, that sets your spirit free.



You'd hear the hum of understanding, deep and warm, a knowing light,

A hand that meets yours gently, through the velvet cloak of night.

You'd hear the echo of forgiveness, washing clean the wounds of strife,

A balm of trust, a whispered prayer, that mends the threads of life.



You'd hear the murmur of encouragement, a firefly's guiding spark,

A gentle nudge, a whispered hope, that sets your wings to mark.

You'd hear the roar of silent pride, a thunder in my beating chest,

For every dream you chase and conquer, every test you dare to crest.



You'd hear the whisper of "I love you," not a shout that tears the air,

But woven like a silken thread, through every note you share.

You'd hear the music of a bond, that time and distance cannot sever,

A lullaby of presence, whispering, "We'll be together, forever."



So if you listen closely, in the hush where dreams unfold,

You'll hear my heart speak volumes, more precious than pure gold.

For in the quiet symphony of sleep, where secrets gently bloom,

My love finds voice, a whispered song, just waiting for your room.
