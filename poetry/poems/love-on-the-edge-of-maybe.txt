Love on the Edge of Maybe

Intro

You trace my hand

Like shifting sand

But every line

Keeps realigning



Verse 1

We talk in circles

Coffee gone cold

You say you're "fine"

Eyes already told

Laugh in the doorway

Like nothing's wrong

Dodging the questions

We've dodged so long



Pre-Chorus

Are we just almost there

Are we just standing near

Why does every heartbeat

Keep asking if you're here



Chorus

Love on the edge of maybe

Halfway brave, halfway scared

I'm falling, but are you falling

Or just standing, staying there

Say you want me, say you don't

I'll be here when shadows blur

Love on the edge of maybe

Hurts the most when it feels like her



Verse 2

You leave your sweater

On my chair

Like some small promise

Never see it through

Paused in the middle

Are we more than two
