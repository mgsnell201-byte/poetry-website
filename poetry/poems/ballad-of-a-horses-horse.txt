Ballad of a Horses Horse

Dust devils whirled, his halo shone,
Young Jim on sun-baked earth alone.
Old Caballo, eyes like flame,
A fiery devil whispered name.

"Leave him be!" the bronc busters spat,
"No hand can tame that Bad Hoss!"
But Jim grinned wide, his heart alight,
"He craves a friend, not endless fight."

Pigeon-toed steps, a whispered plea,
Jim met the mustang's fiery glee.
His roughened palms on sun-kissed mane,
A silent peace that eased the pain.

No whip or spur, just trusting hand,
He spoke of meadows, cool and grand.
Caballo's snort, a trembling sigh,
His fiery heart began to lie.

Then sunrise burst, a dusty blur,
Man and beast, a dance to stir.
Hooves like thunder, laughter's gleam,
Across the plains, a sunlit dream.

He'd spin the devil, light as quail,
Then wink, "See, even fire can fail."
So next time eyes like embers glow,
Remember Jim, where wild hearts flow.
