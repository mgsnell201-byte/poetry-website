Father Frost's Snow-White Crew

Old Father Frost said, "Listen here,
I'm gatherin' polar bears and deer,
the snowy owls with feathers white,
the ermines prowlin' through the night.

"I'll call the hares who hop and play,
the white wolves howlin' far away,
the arctic foxes, sleek and sly—
we'll freeze the world, just them and I!

"We'll frost the fields and chill the air,
we'll sprinkle snowflakes everywhere,
we'll paint the trees with icy gleam,
and freeze up every lake and stream."

"We'll numb your nose and freeze your toes,
we'll even frost your summer clothes!
We'll make the temperature drop low—
we'll bury everything in snow!"

But then one bear said, "Not today,
I'm tired of cold—I'm runnin' away!
I want some warmth and want some sun"—
and all the other creatures run!

Poor Father Frost just stood and glared,
his snow-white army up and scared.
But winter came on time, you see—
it never needed them. Or he.
