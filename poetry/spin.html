Spin

The spider spins her silken thread, The weaver spins the wool to twine, The child's red top spins on its head Until it wobbles out of line. The DJ spins a vinyl track, The bowler spins the ball to bite, The washer spins my laundry stack, The planet spins from day to night. My grandpa spins a fishing tale That grows a foot each time it's told; The pilot fights a spin, gone pale, As engines sputter, stall, and fold. The press aide spins the morning news Until the scandal sounds like praise; At spin class, sweating in my shoes, I pedal nowhere in a daze. Dad takes the Chevy for a spin, An electron spins, though not around; A hit show spawns a spin-off twin, A coin spins ringing on the ground. So when you say "spin," which one is meant: The thread, the lie, the plane's last fall? One word, a hundred ways it's bent, And now my head spins most of all.
