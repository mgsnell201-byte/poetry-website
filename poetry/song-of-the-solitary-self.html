Song of the Solitary Self

I sing the electric song of singular souls, Each breath a universe unfolding in morning mist, Where ancient oaks whisper secrets to wandering hearts, And every footstep marks a path never before taken, The earth beneath acknowledging each unique pressure, Each stride a statement written in dust and dew. Look! There walks a woman gathering wild berries, Her fingers stained purple with summer's abundance, She alone knows the sweetness of this particular moment, The weight of sunlight on her shoulders hers entirely, No other soul can taste what her tongue discovers, No other eyes can see her exact shade of joy. And here a child builds castles from river stones, Each tower leaning at angles known only to him, The water rushing past carries away nothing essential, For what matters lives in the choosing, the placing, The fierce concentration of small hands creating A kingdom that exists because he wills it so. Watch how the old man tends his vegetable garden, Bent but unbroken, teaching tomatoes to climb, His weathered palms hold conversations with soil That began before words, before names were needed, Each plant responds to his particular touch, Growing crooked and beautiful under his care. The mountain stands witness to all our becoming, Silent observer of a million individual dawns, Each sunrise painting the peaks in colors That have never been exactly this way before, And will never be again, though seasons circle, Though the earth spins its ancient, faithful dance. I contain multitudes, yes, but so does the sparrow Picking breadcrumbs from the café table at dawn, So does the teenager sketching in her notebook, Dreaming futures only she can imagine, Her pencil moving to rhythms inside her chest, Creating worlds that pulse with her particular blood. We are each the center of our own expanding universe, Stars wheeling around the gravity of our choices, The wind carries our separate songs across meadows Where wildflowers lean toward their own understanding Of light, of growth, of the wild necessity To bloom exactly as they are meant to bloom. So I celebrate the unrepeatable miracle of you, Standing distinct among seven billion others, Your thoughts branching like rivers toward unknown seas, Your heart beating its singular, irreplaceable rhythm, While around us the world offers its eternal invitation: Be yourself, completely, without apology or fear.
