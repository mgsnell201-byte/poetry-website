The Rooms We Leave

The boxes came on Saturday We lined them by the wall Dad took the pictures down at last And left the shadows tall Her Sunday dress was in the chest Her scarf still smelled of rain My sister pressed it to her face Then folded it again The rooms we leave still hold her voice The floors still know her tread We pack the plates, we wrap the frames We fold the quilt upon her bed No ribbon tied around a box Can hold the years we've known We take the love she left behind And leave the house alone I found her gloves beneath the sink Still stained with garden clay The roses she had tied in spring Kept climbing day by day My brother read her recipe With flour on his sleeve We laughed until the laughter broke And nobody could leave The rooms we leave still hold her voice The floors still know her tread We pack the plates, we wrap the frames We fold the quilt upon her bed No ribbon tied around a box Can hold the years we've known We take the love she left behind And leave the house alone At dusk we sat around her chair And held her wedding band No one could bear to put it down We passed it hand to hand Then quietly Dad sang her song His voice began to shake And through the window roses swayed Like she was still awake The rooms we leave still hold her voice The floors still know her tread We pack the plates, we wrap the frames We fold the quilt upon her bed No ribbon tied around a box Can hold the years we've known We take the love she left behind And leave the house alone I turned the key, we stood outside No words could make it right Her scarf was warm around my neck Like her, it held me tight
