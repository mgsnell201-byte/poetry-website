The Unseen Jig

I heard his coins go chime-a-ling, A metal-tinkle, jig-a-ring! His dancing feet went tap-tap-tap On every rainbow's color-lap. I didn't need a single spark To find the treasure in the dark; I followed where the rhythm fell, A story told in sound and smell. I reached into the emerald mat, Where dew-damp "tickle-tools" sat flat. I felt the squelch of velvet green— The plushiest world that's never seen. A shamrock-scented, dewy balm, It pressed its luck against my palm; A three-leafed, wet, and heavy find, For fingers, not for eyes, designed. The rainbow hummed a bzzz-y tune, A thrumming, vibrating festoon. I pressed my ear against the arc— It crackled violet in the dark! So shut your eyes and hear the jig, The coin-clink-clatter, small and big. The leprechaun won't let you see— But touch and sound—those both are free!
