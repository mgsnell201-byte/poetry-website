Eternal Stand: The Tree of Life

Where ceaseless waves carve cliffs in misty air, The Tree of Life persists, its roots laid bare. Clutching the edge where land and ocean strive, It stands, a quiet force, fiercely alive. What unseen strength upholds its weathered form? Defying storms that rage and tides that swarm. Its branches, gnarled, stretch toward a fleeting dawn, A symbol of resolve where hope is drawn. Through endless cycles, it endures, unswayed, Its roots like truths in earth’s own heart arrayed. No tempest breaks its silent, steadfast grip, A paradox where life and loss equip. Oh, Tree of Life, you speak to souls adrift, Your stubborn stand a timeless, sacred gift. On that wild shore where northern rains descend, You root our fleeting dreams where time transcends.
