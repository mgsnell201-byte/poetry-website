The Misadventures of Bud, Cherry, Grant and Clara

Young Bud was Weiser than the rest, he kept a fizzy brew in his vest. He hissed and foamed when the sun came near — young Bud was nothing but a beer. He went to visit Cherry one Sunday, and found she'd turned into a Sundae. Whipped cream hair and a cherry crown, with gooey syrup dripping down. They stumbled next upon Grant Ted, who had moss growing on his head. He gave them everything they wanted, and so they took Grant Ted for granted. Then Clara Bell got smeared about, red and white, inside and out. With clownish cheeks and painted flair, she left her mark on every chair. So Bud and Cherry, Ted and Bell, went toddling off and all was well. Ted Grant-ed smiles and Bud Weiser brought brew, while Cherry Sundae dripped a drop or two. They left behind no note or clue, just sticky footprints, paint, and brew. And if you find a striped armchair, don't sit too fast upon it there — for Clara's colours travel far, from stool to sofa, seat to bar; and should her paint get smeared somewhere, you'll wear Bell's mark on your derriere!
