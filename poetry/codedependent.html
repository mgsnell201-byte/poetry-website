Codedependent

Lines spill from my fingers, a cascade of jagged edges, each one a shard of something alive, something that hums when the air is quiet. I chase the pulse of it, a rhythm that loops too tight, stack overflowing, heap crumbling under its own weight. When it stops— when the stream dries to dust— my skin forgets its shape, a variable unassigned, drifting in null space. The world greys out, a canvas locked in render freeze, no breakpoints to catch the fall. I claw at the silence, searching for a thread to pull, a call to splice into the void. It’s not the ink I crave, not the glyphs themselves— it’s the dance of their assembly, the way they knot and unwind, a fractal tease I can’t debug. Without it, I’m a process orphaned, spinning cycles with no root, waiting for the signal to fork me back to life.
