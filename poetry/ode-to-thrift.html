Ode To Thrift

In the heart of simplicity, dwells a subtle art, Thrifty by nature's choice, not burdened with excess cart. Each dollar earned and saved with care, Is worth more than gold in mid-air. Banquets less splendid yet richly flavored so sweet Decluttered home where solace and neatness meet. Clothes worn well past seasons' tides Speak volumes about thrift that abounds outside. Value found within pages old and torn, Time-worn toys carry tales since they were born. Discarded trinkets radiate new gleam when repaired – A testament to prudence which others have barely fared. Not driven by consumerism’s ravenous beast - Never lured into feasts for possession increase; Satisfaction springs from depths deep inside - Treasured wants over misdirecting pride, Investing time akin to precious gemstone blue; Saving moments lost amidst routine owing its due- Daily joys savored slowly like an age-old wine- Oh how they shine! That quiet life of thine! So here lies thy hymn thrifty through every line: Cherishing the unpretentious quietly divine! Effulgence permeating all dimensions thick & thin — Praise on thee ' Thrift’, our modest winsome kin!
