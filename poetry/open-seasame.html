Open Seasame

On Sesame Street, where friends all play, A little gate blocked Charlie's way. He tugged and pushed with a worried frown, “I can’t get through—I’m feeling down.” Big Bird saw Charlie's drooping face, “Let’s help our friend find a happy place!” Staring with hopeful heart, “Open Sesame,” he tried to start. Elmo ran up, his giggle bright, “Helping feels good—it’s warm like light!” He held the gate with tiny paws, Pushing hard for Charlie's cause. Cookie Monster munched a treat nearby, “Me help, too!” he said with a sigh. He shared a cookie, soft and sweet, To give Charlie strength for his weary feet. Oscar the Grouch, though grumbly he seems, Hides a soft heart beneath his schemes. He nudged the gate with a dusty broom, While singing his "trashy" tune. Ernie played his saxophone so fun, While Bert said, “Teamwork gets it done!” They pulled together, side by side, Making room for Charlie's stride. Zoe zoomed in, her tutu bold, “Kindness opens gates, I’m told!” She danced and twirled to lift the mood, Showing Charlie that help is good. The Count clapped loud, his numbers clear, “One friend helps, two bring cheer! Three kind hearts, four hands so strong, Together we can not go wrong!” With every push, the gate swung wide, Charlie's smile grew big with pride. “Open Sesame!” they all did sing, Kindness unlocks the happiest thing. On Sesame Street, they learned that day, Helping hands can light the way. When someone’s stuck, just lend a hand, Together, we make life so grand.
