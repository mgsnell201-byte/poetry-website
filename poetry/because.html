'Because'

In hush of dusk, by embers slow, I craved the secrets that did flow, From depths of her obsidian eyes, Where whispered truths and shadows rise. "Why this, my love, why this you choose?" My voice, a thread, the silence loose. And then, she turned, a moonlit grace, A single word upon her face: "Because." No grand decree, no thunderous rhyme, Just that soft curve, that breath of time. A tapestry of reasons wove, In lines of laughter, words of love. "Because the sunrise paints the hill, "Because your hand feels warm and still." "Because the rain speaks to the rose, "Because the fire in darkness glows." "Because we breathe, because we yearn, "Because the stars in orbit turn." "Because the soul has wings to rise, And love's own language speaks in sighs." A breath, a word, "because" alone, A cosmos held in whispered tone. No need for grand decree or tome, Forever's echo finds its home Let starlight hush doubt's fragile plea, In boundless skies, where hearts agree. On cosmic tapestry, souls entwine, Love's echo, endless, yours and mine.
