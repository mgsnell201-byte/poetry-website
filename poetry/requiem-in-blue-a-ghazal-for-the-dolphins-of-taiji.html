Requiem in Blue: A Ghazal for the Dolphins of Taiji

Each year the sea grows quieter, my love, the singers vanish into red, my love. The nets descend like broken promises— what hymns still cling to empty nets, my love? Their eyes hold galaxies we've never mapped, yet we give them knives instead, my love. The cove's embrace is colder than the deep— why do we betray the wet, my love? A mother's grief could drown the rising sun— her child floats where light forgets, my love. They say the hunt is tradition's right, but what of mercy's debt, my love? The tides still whisper never, never— when will we learn what that means, my love?
