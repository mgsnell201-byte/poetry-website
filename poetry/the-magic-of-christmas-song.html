The Magic of Christmas (Song)

The lights are blinking in the trees, each bulb a tiny star, and children press their noses close to windows near and far. Inside a youngster's dreaming mind, the world turns silver-gold, where reindeer truly fly at night through glittering, icy cold. The magic swirls like snowflake dust in every girl and boy— they count the sleeps till Christmas comes and Santa brings them joy. Oh, Christmas light, you shine so bright, through children's eyes and memory's sight, we're keepers of the flame tonight, passing wonder down, holding magic tight. But grown-ups feel it differently, this bittersweet delight, remembering when they believed with all their childish might. We wrap the gifts at midnight's edge, our fingers taped and sore, we string the lights and bake the sweets just like our folks before. There's aching in our happy hearts, a melancholy glow, we're keeping magic alive for them though now we're in the know. And maybe that's the secret gift we never understood— we don't lose Christmas when we grow, we just learn how to give it back, to light the path for those below, becoming stars ourselves at last, the magic-makers in the snow. Oh, Christmas light, you shine so bright, through children's eyes and memory's sight, we're keepers of the flame tonight, passing wonder down, holding magic tight. Yet when we gather 'round the tree, all sparkly, warm, and bright, we're one big family of stars that twinkle through the night. The children gasp at every gleam, the teens pretend they're cool, but everyone still holds the dream— that's Christmas's golden rule. Oh, Christmas light, you shine so bright, through children's eyes and memory's sight, we're keepers of the flame tonight, passing wonder down, holding magic tight.
