Chief Blue Eyed Cat: A Nashian Tale of Feline Conquest

There once was a cat with cerulean sight so keen, Who courted sweet Squaw Pieyed, perpetually preened, Chief Blue Eyed Cat, sleek as silk, sly as sin, Convinced her to marry with a purr and a grin. The tribe held a wedding of feathers and fish, Where Chief Blue Eyed Cat made his one sacred wish: "I'll lead you to glory!" he meowed with great pride, Then promptly curled up for a nap by his bride. His first tribal meeting was held by the creek, Where Chief Blue Eyed Cat gave a speech quite unique: "Meow meow," said the Chief, "and furthermore, purr," The tribe nodded wisely and agreed with a stir. Poor Pieyed, quite pie-eyed from the honeymoon brew, Watched husband catch mice while the tribe's problems grew, The Chief's only counsel was "Nap when you're tired," And "Fish taste much better when properly fired." And that's how a cat with eyes blue as the sea, Became the most powerful chief history'd see, While poets like Nash would have chuckled with glee, At this ridiculous feline monarchy.
