Whispers of Christmas Magic

In the land of winter's delight, A tiny faery spreads Christmas light. Her wings are sparkly, her hair so bright, She flits and flutters with pure delight. With a wand of holly and a heart so true, She brings joy to all, both me and you. She whispers secrets to the snowflakes fair, And makes them dance with a gentle air. Her home is a toadstool, hidden away, Where she keeps her treasures through the winter's day. A stocking filled with berries sweet, And a book of magic, where secrets meet. On Christmas Eve, when all is still, She whispers magic to the silent hill. The stars come out, the moon shines bright, And the Christmas faery's spirit takes flight. So if you look closely on Christmas night, You might just see her, a sparkling light. For the Christmas faery is here to say, "Merry Christmas, dear ones, in a magical way!"
