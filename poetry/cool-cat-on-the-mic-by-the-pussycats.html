Cool Cat on the Mic by the PussyCats

Good evening cats and kittens You're tuned to the grooviest station in town Turn up the hi-fi, kick off your go-go boots 'Cause she's the coolest cat this side of 1965 Shag carpet under my velvet paws A lava lamp is glowing without a cause The hi-fi's spinning my forty-five I'm purring like the radio's come alive I don't need a stage, I don't need a crowd Just a microphone and a sound that's loud My whiskers twitching to the beat I'm the swingingest feline on the street The transistor's humming, the needle's in the groove And when I start to sing, the whole room starts to move I'm a cool cat on the mic (yeah yeah yeah) Singing through the static of the midnight air In a 1960s living room, under the lamp's soft light I'm the queen of the scene, a cool cat on the mic My collar's rhinestone, my fur's a dream I'm the star of a black-and-white TV screen The neighbors knock, they wanna know my tune I'm crooning like a cat on the moon I don't chase mice, I chase melodies A little bit of soul and a whole lot of ease With a wink and a flick of my tail so sly The whole block is saying my oh my The transistor's humming, the needle's in the groove And when I start to sing, the whole room starts to move I'm a cool cat on the mic (yeah yeah yeah) Singing through the static of the midnight air In a 1960s living room, under the lamp's soft light I'm the queen of the scene, a cool cat on the mic When the moon hits the window and the record slows down I curl by the speaker, the sweetest sound in town They say the British are coming, but I'm already here The American kitty with a voice so clear So turn the dial baby, don't you be shy I've got a song that'll never say goodbye I'M A COOL CAT ON THE MIC (yeah yeah yeah) Singing through the static of the midnight air In a 1960s living room, under the lamp's soft light I'm the queen of the scene, a cool cat on the mic I'm a cool cat, cool cat on the mic Yeah Yeah yeah yeah
