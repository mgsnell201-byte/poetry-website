Ripple Effects

In quiet worlds where ripples spread, Our unseen paths by fate are led. Blind to echoes deeds bestow, Tethered by threads we scarcely know. Amid our mirrored, selfish gaze, The sky’s vast hues and twilight’s blaze. Whispers in the rustling trees, Show how our steps shape distant seas. Each whisper, smile, and frown we cast, Affects lives, both near and vast. Butterfly wings in distant air, Shift tides and sands with tender care. But what if in the still of mind, We embraced love for all mankind? Saw earth, sky, and sea as one, A boundless web, never to be undone. Be the beacon in dark of night, Drying tears, letting hope alight. With hearts so pure and intentions true, We'll craft a world for me and you. Trace your steps along the shore, Each footfall marks what came before. Change begins with a single stride, Together, we'll shape the rising tide.
