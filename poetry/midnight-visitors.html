Midnight Visitors

In dead of night, a sound so near, Jolts me from slumber, gripped by fear. What spectral force now haunts these halls? What ghostly hand upon me calls? With trembling hand, I light my way, Down winding stairs, in shadows grey. Each creak and groan, a scary sign, Of horrors lurking by design. My heart pounds fast, my breath grows short, As shadows dance and fears contort. What waits on ground floor? A ghost? A ghoul? Or something more. The noise grows louder as I near, I gather courage, fight my fear. With trembling hand, I reach the door, And find a sight I can't ignore. For there, beneath the moon so bright, Stand little ones, a cheerful sight. With bags held high and faces beaming, "Trick or treat!" their voices streaming. Small witches, goblins, and cartoon heroes, Pirates, princesses, and tiny Nero's, All giggling, skipping, full of glee, No scary monsters, just kids I see. My racing heart begins to slow, As laughter fills the night's soft glow. The fright that gripped me starts to fade, Replaced by joy at this parade. I chuckle at my foolish dread, The monsters were all in my head. For Halloween's true magic lies, In children's smiles and sparkling eyes.
