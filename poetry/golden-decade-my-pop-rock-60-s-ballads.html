Golden Decade: My Pop, Rock & 60’s Ballads

(Imagine these lyrics sung to the rhythm of Queen "We Will Rock You") Verse 1 SURF guitars—and sunshine sound— BEACH Boys dreams—palm trees ground— "Good Vibra-TIONS"—love found— Sweet harmo-NIES—all around— Verse 2 MAR-ty's voice—cowboy tales— "EL Pa-SO"—lone star trails— "BIG I-RON"—desert gales— Western TRUTH—never fails— Pre-Chorus SI-mon's words—soft and clear— "SOUND of Si-LENCE"—in my ears— "MIS-sus Rob-IN-son" appears— Then FREDDIE belts—brings the cheers— CHORUS POP! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —The SIX-ties gave it SOUL! ROCK! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —Queen turned UP the GOLD! BAL-LADS! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —Mar-ty's STO-ries TOLD! THESE ARE THE SOUNDS— THAT NEV-ER GET OLD! Bridge "GOD On-ly KNOWS"—what dreams may come— "KILL-er QUEEN"—with lightning strum— FROM folk to FAME—the decades run— But OLD songs STILL—strike like drums— Final Chorus POP! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —The RA-dios still PLAY! ROCK! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —It NEV-er fades a-WAY! BAL-LADS! (Stomp-Stomp-CLAP) —They HAUNT me ev-ery DAY! THESE ARE THE SOUNDS— THE SIX-TIES MADE!
