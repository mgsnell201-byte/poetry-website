Across the Room

Her eyes catch his across the room, A spark that lights the trembling air. She lingers in the shadows gloom, Too shy to cross, too small to dare. His laugh, a breeze through open doors, Stirs dreams she locks in silent doubt. Her heart, a flicker barred by shores, Fears glances meet and spark will rout. The crowd hums low, a restless tide, Yet he’s a shore she’ll never reach. Her thoughts, like embers, glow and hide, Each step a word she cannot speak. What if his glance should meet her own? The risk of hurt looms sharp and near. She stays, a statue carved of stone, And lets the moment disappear.
