No Words of Mine

You stayed tonight to ease the pain But I can feel what still remains Don't tell me things you don't believe Just hold me close until you leave And if I ask you why don't explain No words of mine will ever make you stay I can't bring back the love that slipped away I know this borrowed night is all you'll give I'll take it, though I don't know how I'll live You're here with me, but miles away No words of mine will ever make you stay I'll close my eyes and just pretend That holding you won't have to end When morning comes, I'll let you go There's nothing left to say, we both know But part of me won't let go No words of mine will ever make you stay I can't bring back the love that slipped away I know this borrowed night is all you'll give I'll take it, though I don't know how I'll live You're here with me, but miles away No words of mine will ever make you stay Maybe love doesn't always need a reason Maybe this was all the time we were given And morning is coming in to take its due I'll let you go the way that mornings do No words of mine will ever make you stay I can't bring back the love that slipped away I know that this borrowed night is all you'll give I'll take it though I don't know how I'll live You're here with me but miles away No words of mine will ever make you stay Our borrowed night has turned to day I watch you turn and walk away There's nothing left for me to say No words of mine will ever make you stay
