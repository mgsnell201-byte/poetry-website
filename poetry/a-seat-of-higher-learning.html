A Seat of Higher Learning

Your Hogwarts owl vanished, lost in the mail, but fear not, young wizard—we won't let you fail! No wand or charmed cauldron, no cloak spun with glee, we've conjured you a toilet seat for your bathroom's decree! Now don't look so puzzled, it's perfectly planned, this magical seat was crafted by hand. It's enchanted with spells from our finest prefects, and comes with some truly crappy side effects. This seat really runs on the finest of magic, though sometimes results can be quite dramatic. We aim to please with each sitting spell, but this cushioned throne has stories to tell. The hinges may squeak with a mystical tune, the seat might just glow beneath a full moon. When nature calls and you need to go, this seat knows exactly which way the winds blow. So welcome to magic, though not what you sought, this toilet seat's worth more than you thought. In the most important room when push comes to shove, this seat will give you the support that you love!
