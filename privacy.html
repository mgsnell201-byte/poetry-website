Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of how this site handles your information.

What We Collect

This site does not use advertising, analytics trackers, or tracking cookies. When you submit a message using a "Leave a Note" form, the name, optional email address, and comment you enter are sent through Web3Forms solely to deliver your message to the site owner's inbox.

How It Is Used

Your email address is never required, never sold or shared with third parties, and never added to any mailing list. If you provide an email, it is used only to reply to your note. To request deletion of a previously submitted note, send a message through any "Leave a Note" form on the site.
