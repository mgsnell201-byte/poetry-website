Site Information

Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of how this site handles your information.

What We Collect

This site does not use advertising, analytics trackers, or tracking cookies. When you submit a message using a "Leave a Note" form, the name, optional email address, and comment you enter are sent directly to the site owner's inbox solely to deliver your message to the site owner's inbox.

How It Is Used

Notes are not stored in a database on this website—they are simply delivered to the site owner's email inbox so they can read or reply to your message. Your email address is never required, never sold or shared with third parties, and never added to any mailing list. If you prefer not to use the "Leave a Note" form, you can email marvin@osmoticpoet.com directly.
