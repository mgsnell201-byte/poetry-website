Artist Bio

About Marvin Snell (OsmoticPoet)

My name is Marvin Snell, and I created this site to share my original poetry and music. I have been writing both for over eight years, have had two poems published, and share work on AllPoetry under the pseudonym OsmoticPoet. Every poem and song lyric here is written entirely by me, with no AI used in the writing process.

Studio & Process

Artwork & Music Production

While every word is mine, I use AI as a creative tool for the visuals and studio production: Cover Art & Video: I design each visual by describing the scene, mood, and composition in words—drawing with language instead of a pencil—and use AI to bring that direction to life. Music: Without a physical studio, session musicians, or vocalists, I bring my lyrics into ComfyUI, where I direct the key, time signature, instrumentation, and vocal character. From there, I mix and master every track myself in Fender Studio Pro before publishing it here for your listening pleasure.
