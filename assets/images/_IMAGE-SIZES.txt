OsmoticPoet — Cover Image Size Cheat Sheet
============================================

POEMS:
  Size:   896 x 1120 px  (4:5 portrait)
  Format: .jpg (or .png — publish-poetry.bat auto-converts .png to .jpg)

MUSIC (YouTube):
  Size:   1280 x 720 px  (16:9 landscape)
  Format: .jpg (or .png — publish-music.bat auto-converts .png to .jpg)

Naming Rule:
  - Lowercase with hyphens, no spaces (e.g. first-sip, winter-s-first-lesson, tribal-beat)
  - Must match the poem/song filename before the extension.
  - Drop replacement images for existing poems/songs right here in assets\images\
    (if replacing a .jpg with a .png or vice versa, delete the old one),
    then run publish-poetry.bat or publish-music.bat.
